EPISODE 4 - RAW TRANSCRIPT
==========================

WhisperX output from the session recording, with speaker diarisation.
Source material. None of this is published to the website: the site builder
only reads .md files, so everything in this folder lives in the repository
only. It is here so that players and the DM can work from the same source
instead of passing files around.

FILES
-----
  episode-04.txt      the readable transcript, one line per utterance,
                      prefixed with the speaker tag
  episode-04.srt      the same content with timestamps, so it can be played
                      alongside the audio in any media player
  speaker_map.json    which SPEAKER_NN tag belongs to which person

The word-level .json and the .vtt are deliberately not committed. The .json
is roughly 6 MB per episode and the .vtt duplicates the .srt.

THREE THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE YOU TRUST IT
----------------------------------------
1. TWO LINES ARE REDACTED. Nobody said a real name out of character in this
   session, which is a first. Two lines of out-of-character chat did identify
   where a player lives, and both now read "[out of character, redacted]".
   Nothing else has been altered.

2. ONE TAG IS A BIN, NOT A PERSON. SPEAKER_05 is mapped to UNCERTAIN because it
   is not one speaker: it collects lines from several people, and the opening
   round of introductions in particular is attributed almost at random.
   SPEAKER_04 also picks up the GM occasionally. Read the speaker tags as a
   hint, not a fact.

   WhisperX also mangles proper nouns throughout. In this episode: "Laura" and
   "Larg" are Lark; "Kaldr", "Kaldor", "Golda" and "Colder" are Calder; "Asuka",
   "Asker", "Aske" and "Eska" are Aeska; "William" is Billiam; "Hal" and "Hall"
   are Harl; "Clarence Bowen", "Clarence Bone", "Clarence Burns" and "Climbing
   Bronze" are all Claringbold's Yard; "Bookman" and "Bergman" are Buckman;
   "Shrepnel" is Shrapnel; "Spernhold" is Spurnhold; "Fixed Field" and "Fax
   Field" are Fexfield; "Portis Guild" is the Porter's Guild; "Pull and Bob"
   and "Pum and Bob" are the Plumb and Bob. "Paul Calder" in one line is a
   mis-transcribed filler sound, not a person. Check names against the wiki
   rather than copying them out of here.

3. THE SPEAKER MAP IS PER-EPISODE. WhisperX assigns SPEAKER_00, SPEAKER_01 and
   so on in the order it happens to find voices, so the numbering is different
   in every episode and carries no meaning across them. Always read the
   speaker_map.json sitting in the same folder as the transcript you are using.

Per the Canon & Discipline rules, the transcript is the only source of truth
for what happened in a session. That still holds. Just read it knowing the
labels are approximate even where the words are not.
