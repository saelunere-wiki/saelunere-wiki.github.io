# Episode 4 - What the Ledger Says

- **Date played:** 13 September 2026 / Tide 02 into Tide 03, 756
- **In attendance:** Aeska, Billiam, Calder, Felix, Lark
- **In brief:** A day of consequences. [[Harriet Spurnhold]] chooses to disappear, [[Barry Killerman]] has been taken, [[Shrapnel]] has been taken, and the ledger the party nearly died for has been almost entirely destroyed by its own author.

## Key events

*Midnight in the Bellows, then one full day, played mostly one character at a
time until they converge in the afternoon.*

### Getting out

- **Lark handed the ledger to Felix and threw up** once the adrenaline dropped, having nearly died twice in a day.
- **She rounded on Calder** for chasing the shooter instead of helping. He said it was a reaction, that the man was the one piece of the puzzle worth catching. She told him he is no use to anyone dead.
- **[[Harriet Spurnhold]] left the party.** The ambush was meant for her, she will bring danger wherever she goes, and she has friends on the far side of the city. She can be reached by Sending and no other way.
- **They split up for the night.** Felix took a random tavern, Calder his College dorm, Billiam went home, Aeska went to check on [[The Plumb and Bob]], Lark went to the bathhouse.

### Aeska, at the Plumb and Bob

- **The Porter's Guild came back and took [[Barry Killerman]]**, the only person they could lay hands on after the fight in the square. [[Harl Mott]] has not seen him since.
- Harl knows nothing about the two beyond the green coats. Barry did not recognise them either.
- **Harl issued a warning.** Twice in two nights is starting to cost him business. Friendly, but a warning.

### Lark, at the bathhouse

- **She arrived after midnight covered in blood** at **Salador's**, a bathhouse in [[The Stacks]] heated off the essence pipework under the street. The doorman, **Emery**, turned her away and caught her easily when she tried to dash past.
- **Delia** let her in on sufferance. Lark cleaned the entire bathhouse through the small hours and **took a point of exhaustion** for it, then slept on the floor of **Nell**'s room.
- **[[Madame Rook]] runs the place.** **Natural 20:** she is something like a crime lord who commits no crime. She trades in information and favours, holds dirt on a great many people, and has real sway in this part of the Stacks.
- **She already knew how Lark had arrived.** She asked where Lark was from, what she did before couriering, and why she turns up at bathhouses at midnight covered in blood. Lark gave her [[Brassline]] and said she wants nothing to do with [[The Porter's Guild]]. Rook knows [[Mira]] by name.
- **The job.** Rook wants confirmation that a certain fallen nobleman's son, **Aeska Volan**, has been seen at the Plumb and Bob, performs there, or is known to its regulars, plus anything else Lark can bring. A place at the bathhouse and a reward if she does.
  - **And a threat.** Lark owes a debt for the intrusion, and if she is not diligent, her friend Nell will answer for having sheltered a "potential fugitive". Report back by tomorrow.
- **Lark told Nell immediately** that they had both been threatened, and told Aeska to his face later the same day.

### Billiam, at the yard

- **[[Claringbold's Yard]] was empty.** The gate unlatched, no workers, and **[[Shrapnel]] gone**. It was a normal working day.
- **Both constructs had been attacked.** **Shepherd** met him limping on three legs; **Steam Bun** was on its side venting steam with one leg still turning. Billiam named them after shepherd's pie and steamed buns.
- **The office had been stripped**, drawers pulled out, the secure cash drawer broken open and emptied. No blood, and no way to tell whether there had been a fight or just a search.
- He spent the morning trying and failing to repair them.

### The ledger

- **Almost all of it has been destroyed.** Four or five pages of two-column entries have been **deliberately blotted out with ink**, both columns, permanently.
- **Two entries survive**, the last on the most recent page. The left column is in **Pavosi** and names a place; the right is in **Technic** and gives serial numbers and a quantity.
  - **Fexfield Scrappers - x50**
  - **[[Claringbold's Yard]] - x30**
- **Aeska does not recognise the serial numbers at all**, which surprises him. He should know most of what [[The Guild of Engineers]] deals with.

### At the yard, together

- **Billiam knows one of the serial numbers.** Shrapnel pointed it out to him weeks ago on a machine nobody had seen before, something like a new generation of design, which they stripped together.
- **Natural 20:** it held **fifteen essence cores**, Shrapnel said at the time that they were **odd and more powerful than normal**, and he took the box away and Billiam never saw it again. A **second machine of the same type** came in later. Fifteen and fifteen makes thirty, which is what the ledger says.
- **Felix asked the construct.** Aeska could not repair either of them, so Felix cast Speak with Machines as a ritual on Shepherd, with a bardic inspiration from Aeska. What it gave him:
  - *"Big bad time. Bang, bang, bang."*
  - The people were **"big, green coat, blonde man, very fast"** - the monk from [[Oblong Square]], the one Billiam spat at.
  - **"Boss taken, yes."** And of the office, *"smash, smash."*
- **So the Porter's Guild took Shrapnel.** Separately, the people who had been leaning on the yard for extra rent were [[The Corewright's Association]], who own it. Two different guilds.

### Billiam and Aeska

- **Aeska left the house** because his being there would not have helped whatever was happening between Billiam and his father.
- **Aeska's father is Valoran Volan**, and he worked closely with [[Professor Benjamin Buckman]] on projects neither man would discuss with anybody, including their sons.
- **Billiam has been through his father's things** and found papers marked with a **V**, and they were recent rather than two years old. The two fathers may still be working together.
- Billiam told Aeska he had been instructed to turn himself in and to hand Aeska over, and that he had said yes and meant no.
- Aeska: he was raised to succeed [[Dr. Mannsfield T. Tammerton|Dr. Tammerton]], refused it, and is a disgrace to his family for refusing.

### Calder's letter

- **[[Professor Benjamin Buckman]] wrote back.** Most of it is ordinary supervision, answering Calder's research questions.
- The postscript, where Calder had asked about powerful essence weapons, gets one paragraph: **"I recommend that you stop this line of inquiry."** It is not relevant to his College duties, and the College does not delve into the aggressive application of magic.

### The back room

- **They confronted Calder.** Lark took him by both hands and asked whether they could trust him. Aeska asked why he had chased the shooter instead of helping.
- **Calder's answer:** Felix looked to be holding, the shooter was the only person there who knew anything, and he could not tell whether the man was fleeing or taking cover to fire again.
- **Aeska read him as telling the entire truth.** Calder is cleared, for now.
- **Billiam told Calder that Buckman is his father**, which Calder had not known. Calder produced the letter in return.
- **The Plumb and Bob is compromised.** They agreed to find a new meeting place, somewhere nearer the mouth of [[The Bellows]] where the guilds go less.
- **Tomorrow's plan.** Billiam checks the yard at nine, then meets Felix at **Fexfield Scrappers** in [[The Core|the Core]] at ten. Aeska, Lark and Calder look for a way into the Porter's Guild tower, where they believe Shrapnel is being held.
- **Calder asked around about the Porter's Guild** that evening. Most people know nothing. A few made it clear **they would rather not talk about them.**
- **Aeska performed as usual** so as not to draw attention by stopping, on a **natural 20**, and started thinking about changing his appearance.
- **Lark went back to Salador's to talk to Madame Rook.** The session ends before that conversation.

**Calder, Felix and Lark levelled up**, for surviving the Bellows. Aeska and Billiam did not.

## NPCs, locations, factions

- [[Madame Rook]] - runs Salador's bathhouse in the Stacks. Older, salt and pepper, half-moon spectacles, rings. Trades information and favours rather than committing crimes, and is not to be crossed.
- **Nell** - works at the bathhouse, Lark's age and her friend there. Now unknowingly leverage.
- **Delia** - the senior of the bathhouse workers. Let Lark in and covered for her.
- **Emery** - the bathhouse doorman. Young, does not look like a bouncer, and is much stronger than he looks.
- [[Harriet Spurnhold]] - gone, and reachable only by Sending.
- [[Barry Killerman]] - **taken by the Porter's Guild** the night of the fight in the square.
- [[Shrapnel]] - **taken from his own yard**, along with every other worker there.
- **Shepherd and Steam Bun** - the yard's two bullyboy [[Constructs|constructs]], both damaged in the raid. Shepherd lost a leg and can still walk; Steam Bun cannot get up. Shepherd is the only witness the party has.
- [[Professor Benjamin Buckman]] - told Calder in writing to drop the subject of essence weapons, and is possibly still working with Aeska's father.
- **Valoran Volan** - Aeska's father, second to Dr. Tammerton. Worked closely with Buckman on undisclosed projects.
- **Fexfield Scrappers** - a scrapyard in the Core, far bigger and more industrial than Claringbold's, twenty or thirty staff. The other name in the ledger.
- [[The Corewright's Association]] - owns Claringbold's Yard and licences it. Not the people who raided it.
- [[The Porter's Guild]] - has now taken two people, and holds prisoners in its own tower rather than any civic prison.

## Loot, contacts, consequences

- **The ledger is nearly worthless.** Theo inked out every entry but the last two himself. What survives is two scrapyards and two quantities.
- **Thirty unusual essence cores passed through Claringbold's Yard**, stronger than standard, and Shrapnel took them somewhere.
- **Shrapnel and Barry Killerman are both in Porter's Guild hands**, along with the yard's other five or six workers.
- **Lark has a debt to Madame Rook** and a deadline of tomorrow, and the price of failing is Nell.
- **Lark has told Aeska** that someone is paying for confirmation of where he is.
- **Billiam's job is gone**, at least for now, and his employer with it.
- **Calder has been warned off in writing** by his own supervisor.
- **The party is short of a safe meeting place.** Harl has asked them to keep their trouble out of his bar.
