# Episode 8 - At the Wrong Hour

- **Date played:** 8 October 2026 / Tide 05 into Tide 06, 756
- **In attendance:** Aeska, Billiam, Calder, Felix, Lark
- **In brief:** Three errands and a disaster. Calder robs his supervisor's office, Alfie gives up half of what came out of Shrapnel's safe, and Billiam walks in on his mother.

## Key events

*The evening of Episode 7, then the following day.*

### Calder at the College

- **He broke into [[Professor Benjamin Buckman]]'s office.** He felt what he took for an alarm spell, so kept it under ten minutes.
- **Took a secondment notice**: the [[The College of Arcanographers|College]] releasing Buckman to [[The Guild of Engineers]] temporarily. Aeska's read later was that it tells them nothing they did not have.
- **Took a set of blueprints** - technical drawings for essence machinery. **Natural 1** on the engineering check; they mean nothing to him.
- **Left a wrapped package**, book-sized, as too awkward to carry out.
- **He knocked a ladder over**, and the College secretary came up. She used Detect Magic, saw the shape of an invisible man, and cast Hold Person on him. He shook it off and got out by Misty Step through a window.

### Lark at Brassline

- **[[Denton]] let her in.** [[Mira]] talked, with the warning to be careful.
- **On Evan Langford:** he runs his own crew doing off-the-books work and smuggling; everyone in the guild knows and nothing is done, because he is the head's son. He lives in the family estates at the top of the Porter's spire.
- **What he actually cares about** is his name. No black marks, ambitions of running the guild one day, and he is not short of money.
- **On Gertrude:** a spellcaster high up in the guild who does not run deliveries any more.
- **A name surfaced:** *Kertrid Marin*. Nothing has gone to them from Brassline.

### The morning

- **Felix woke at Billiam's house** and was fed breakfast by [[Annie Buckman|Annie]], who had not known he was there.
- **Billiam hid the scar under extra clothing** and left as though going to work. He has no work to go to.
- **They split up.** Aeska and Billiam to [[Alfie]]; Calder, Felix and Lark to deliver Madame Rooke's letter.

### The Tilbury letter

- **Mrs Tilbury would not read it first** and called her husband to the door.
- **Lark opened it and read it aloud**: a complaint from the bathhouse that **Amos Tilbury had been rough with Mary**. He lunged, took it off her and crumpled it, and told his wife Lark was a scammer after money.
- **Felix stepped into an alley and turned into a [[Constructs|howl]].** Calder watched him do it and is the first of them to see the change itself rather than the aftermath. Amos backed off from the growling.
- **Lark's parting line** was that she never wants to see his face in Salador's Slip again.

### Alfie

- **He let Billiam in and admitted it.** He and Hollis took a box out of the safe, on Hollis's reasoning that they were owed wages and Shrapnel was not coming back. **They split it between them.**
- **Aeska cast on him.** Alfie bolted, went over the rooftops, and got away into the steam of the Stacks.
- **Billiam searched and found a linen sack with ten modified essence cores in it.** Sold as ordinary cores they would fetch about three hundred ducats, and they are not ordinary.
- **Billiam rounded on Aeska** for casting behind his back, and for costing them the one person who would have talked.
- **Hollis still has the other half.**

### Billiam goes home

- **He found [[Denton]] in bed with his mother.** Annie told him he was supposed to be at work and tried to move him out of the room.
- **He cast Command on her**, then went at Denton with his fists. Denton covered up, did not try to hurt him, and hit back when nothing else stopped it.
- **The other three came in.** Lark took a punch meant for Denton. Calder and Lark tried to get between them and were pushed past.
- **Aeska cast Silence on the room**, and it became a soundless brawl: four people hitting each other, Calder shouting with nothing coming out.
- **Then he dropped it and held Denton**, while Felix held Billiam. Both froze, the silence lifted, and Calder was still shouting into a room that had gone quiet.

**The session ends there.**
