# Episode 8 - At the Wrong Hour

- **Date played:** 7 October 2026 / Tide 05 into Tide 06, 756
- **In attendance:** Aeska, Billiam, Calder, Felix, Lark
- **In brief:** Three errands and a disaster. Calder robs his supervisor's office, Alfie gives up ten of the cores that came out of Shrapnel's safe, and Billiam walks in on his mother.

## Key events

*The evening of Episode 7, then the following day.*

### Calder at the College

- **He broke into [[Professor Benjamin Buckman]]'s office.** He sensed what he took for an alarm spell on the door, so kept it under ten minutes. The office was half empty.
- **Took a secondment agreement**: [[The College of Arcanographers|the College]] releasing Buckman to [[The Guild of Engineers]], signed for the Guild by *"V.V., Head of Operations"*. See [[The Secondment Agreement]].
- **Took a set of blueprints** - technical drawings for essence machinery. They meant nothing to him.
- **Left a wrapped package**, book-sized, as too awkward to carry out, **and a College file**.
- **He knocked a ladder over**, and the College secretary came up. She sensed the magic on him as he slipped past, shouted *"Intruder!"* and tried to hold him with a spell, which he shook off.
- **He got away by a bluff.** He stepped up the lift shaft to the floor above, opened his notebook and played the bystander. She saw his face, believed him, and sent him to his room while she locked the spire down.
- **By morning** the students had gossip, and no leads or suspects that he heard of.

### Lark at Brassline

- **[[Denton]] let her in.** [[Mira]] talked, with the warning to be careful.
- **On Evan Langford:** he runs his own crew doing off-the-books work and smuggling; everyone in the guild knows and nothing is done, because he is the head's son. He lives in the family estates at the top of the Porters' spire.
- **What he actually cares about** is his name. No black marks, ambitions of running the guild one day, and he is not short of money.
- **On [[Gertrude]]:** a spellcaster high up in the guild who does not run deliveries any more. **Her surname is Marin**, and her son is [[Alistar]]. Nothing has gone to them from Brassline.
- **Lark and Aeska talked that night.** She apologised for pushing him to show himself, and asked about his family. He said he has told them what he can, and that there are things he does not share. He cannot go on paying for her room.

### The morning

- **Felix woke at Billiam's house.** [[Annie Buckman|Annie]], who had not known he was there, offered him breakfast, which he declined, and told him he is welcome any time.
- **At [[Fexfield Scrappers]]** his pay is one mark a morning. The two marks of his first shift were a starting bonus.
- **Billiam hid the scar under extra clothing** and left as though going to work. He has no work to go to. He waited for his mother to leave, then **searched her room** and found nothing.

### At the Cobbler's Pot

- **Calder handed Aeska the agreement**: *"I think this is your father's signature down there."* So the Head of Operations at the Guild of Engineers is [[Valerin Volan]].
- **Calder apologised to Billiam** for going through his father's office. Billiam: *"these last few weeks, most of you have treated me better than my father had."*
- **The blueprints were read.** Aeska reads Technic. **Billiam knew the drawing: the machine he and [[Shrapnel]] took apart.** One serial number matches one in Theo's ledger. It is an engine, a great many prototype cores set together to make a huge amount of power in a small space. See [[The Blueprints]].
- **They split up.** Aeska and Billiam to [[Alfie]]; Lark to deliver Madame Rooke's letter, with Calder and Felix watching from down the street.

### The Tilbury letter

- **Mrs Tilbury would not read it first** and called her husband to the door.
- **Lark opened it and read it aloud**: a complaint from the bathhouse that **Amos Tilbury had been rough with Mary**. He lunged, took it off her and crumpled it, and told his wife Lark was a scammer after money.
- **Felix stepped into an alley and turned into a [[Constructs|howl]].** Calder watched him do it and is the first of them to see the change itself rather than the aftermath. Amos backed off from the growling.
- **Lark barred him from [[Salador's Slip]]**: she never wants to see his face there again, and the house has guard howls.
- **Behind the closing door**, Mrs Tilbury: *"we're going to be having a talk about your nightly activities."*

### Alfie

- **He let Billiam in and admitted it.** It was Hollis's idea: they were owed wages and Shrapnel was not coming back, and Hollis knew where the safe was. They took a box of cores out of it and **split it between them.**
- **Aeska, outside, put a compulsion to tell the truth on the room.** Alfie: **Hollis has not sold his share. He is keeping it at [[The Firebox]], and looking for a buyer.** Then he realised what he was saying, bolted through the skylight, and got away over the rooftops into the steam of the Stacks.
- **Billiam searched and found a linen sack with ten prototype cores in it.** Sold as ordinary cores they would fetch about three hundred ducats, and they are not ordinary.
- **Billiam rounded on Aeska** for casting behind his back, and for costing them the one person who would have talked.
- **They did not go on to Hollis.** Aeska expects Alfie to warn him. Lark and Calder wanted to get there first, and Billiam said no: they have what they came for.

### Billiam goes home

- **He found [[Denton]] in bed with his mother.** Annie told him he was supposed to be at work and tried to move him out of the room.
- **He cast Command on her**, then went at Denton with his fists. Denton covered up, did not try to hurt him, and hit back when nothing else stopped it.
- **The other three came in.** Lark took a punch meant for Denton. Calder and Lark tried to get between them and were pushed past.
- **Aeska picked up the sack of cores** Billiam had dropped at the door and put it away. Felix saw him do it.
- **Aeska cast Silence on the room**, and it became a soundless brawl: four people hitting each other, Calder shouting with nothing coming out.
- **Then he dropped it and held Denton**, while Felix held Billiam. Both froze, the silence lifted, and Calder was still shouting into a room that had gone quiet.

**The session ends there.**

## NPCs, locations, factions

- **[[Denton]]** - works at Brassline, let Lark in, and is sleeping with Annie Buckman. Took a beating without seriously fighting back.
- **[[Annie Buckman]]** - found out by her son at the worst possible moment.
- **[[Alfie]]** - admitted the theft, ran, and is now somewhere in the Stacks knowing the party came for him.
- **[[Hollis]]** - keeping his share of the cores at the Firebox and looking for a buyer. Aeska expects Alfie to warn him.
- **[[Mira]]** - talked freely about Evan Langford and told Lark to be careful.
- **The College secretary** - a capable caster. She did not catch Calder, but she knows somebody invisible was in the offices, and she saw him one floor above.
- **Amos Tilbury** - took the letter off Lark, called her a scammer, and backed away from a howl.
- **[[Gertrude]] and [[Alistar]] Marin** - the two Porter's Guild agents now have a surname.

## Loot, contacts, consequences

- **Ten prototype cores**, in a sack, taken from Alfie. Aeska has them.
- **A secondment agreement** putting Buckman at the Guild of Engineers, and **a set of blueprints** for the machine Billiam took apart.
- **Somebody at the College knows the offices were robbed** by an invisible caster.
- **Rooke's letter was delivered**, read aloud in the street, and destroyed.
- **Alfie is loose** and has every reason to warn Hollis.
