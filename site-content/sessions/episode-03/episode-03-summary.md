# Episode 3 - The Ledger

- **Date played:** 4 September 2026 / Tide 02, 756
- **In attendance:** Aeska, Billiam, Calder, Felix, Lark
- **In brief:** The fight in [[Oblong Square]] is survived, not won. The party goes to ground at Billiam's mother's house, where his father comes home for the first time in days and tells him to turn himself in. At midnight in the [[The Bellows|Bellows]], [[Harriet Spurnhold]] explains what she took from [[Theodore Blackwood|Theo]]'s house and is shot before she can finish the sentence.

## Key events

*One continuous evening, picking up exactly where Episode 2 stopped, and
splitting into two scenes for the second half.*

### Oblong Square

- **The scarred Aela man is a monk.** He crossed the square at double speed, caught Lark from behind and hit her once in the back of the head. Ten points of damage, and a **stunning strike**: no movement, no action, no reaction.
- **Then he put a rope around her neck.** A length pulled off his own belt, looped over her head from behind, another eighteen points of damage, and every intention of dragging her backwards with it. Lark, half blacked out, asked him whether he was going to kill her just for a name. **He looked her in the eyes and said nothing.**
- **Felix's first spell is counterspelled.** He tried to hold the monk, knocked over barrels coming out of the alley, and the woman in the green coat turned and shut the spell down before it landed.
- **Calder shouts for the crowd.** He cast mage armour on himself and called out that these people were beating up an innocent person. It worked well enough: merchants, washerwomen and porters stopped to look. Nobody stepped in, because the two attackers were in uniform and nobody knew who was in the right, but **the square was watching**, and Calder saw the woman in green register that.
- **Aeska's music takes the woman out of the fight.** From the mouth of the alley he played a chorus that stopped her where she stood, incapacitated, listening. He put an inspiring flourish on the end of it for Lark.
- **Lark gets out of the rope.** She healed herself, then argued the escape should be dexterity rather than strength, and got it: a twist off the monk's legs, a judo throw out of the coil, and away through the crowd with an opportunity attack behind her.
- **The second Hold Person lands.** Felix cast it again and the monk froze mid-stride, one step into a chase, **eyes locked on Felix** as the spell took him.
- **Billiam spits in his face.** Having come out of the front door and spent the whole fight running, he passed the paralysed monk at a dead sprint and spat at him on the way by. The monk could not move, or blink, or do anything about it.
- **They get clear.** Two minutes of running at full speed with nobody behind them, deeper into [[The Stacks]].

### On the run

- **Lark apologises to Aeska.** She gave up no names, not his and not Felix's, and said so. She also pointed out that [[Barry Killerman]] was left sitting in that tavern as the only person who had been talking to her. Aeska: he is not really their problem any more.
- **The pins were guild pins, not crew pins.** Lark knows [[The Porter's Guild]] structure: a large network of crews moving goods, and a lot of influence because everybody needs things moved. **Natural 20.** The two who came for her were not a rival crew. They were wearing the guild's own pins, which puts them at **the level above the crews**, close to the top.
- **[[Brassline]] is a Porter's Guild crew.** Lark told the others, unprompted, that the depot she worked at until yesterday sits under the same guild. She also remembers occasional visits to the Brassline offices by people in green coats like those, and that [[Mira]] stayed well clear of guild politics, paid her dues and had no ambitions beyond that.

### Billiam's house

- **He takes them home.** Up the ladders and ramps of the Stacks to a small, well kept, three-room house built flush against a chimney for the warmth, high enough for a view across the whole city. The doors on the balcony have numbers, not names. About nine in the evening.
- **[[Annie Buckman]], Billiam's mother.** Washing up after dinner, and delighted. She put the kettle on, shook everyone's hand, apologised for her son's manners, told him off for not introducing his friends, and insisted they help themselves to the mint she grows herself.
- **He asks her to leave the room.** **Natural 20**, with advantage, because it is his mother. She went out to water the plants, came back once for the watering can, and went out again.
- **Aeska asks why the change of heart.** Billiam had been secretive with him for two days and had now brought them all home. His answer: he did not know who to trust, and it does not matter, because trouble is coming to them anyway. Aeska apologised for casting on him in Episode 2 and it was accepted. Aeska also admitted, without detail, that he has spent two years hiding.
- **Correction: it was the Corewrights at the scrapyard.** The people who have been leaning on [[Shrapnel]] at [[Claringbold's Yard]] and taxing him extra were **[[The Corewright's Association]]**, not the Porter's Guild. The Corewrights make and keep the essence cores, and while they are their own guild they generally do what [[The Guild of Engineers]] tells them.
- **What an essence core costs.** Billiam strips them for a living: **twenty to thirty ducats each**, sometimes more. Ten of them in a box is more than a year's wage. Aeska adds that during his guild years there were standing experimental projects on making cores more efficient, and on **wiring many of them together to see what happened**.
- **Which lines up with [[Orson Thorne]]'s estimate.** A weapon that throws an essence blast that far and still hits needs a great deal of power. A great deal of power means a great many cores, which means a great deal of money and access.
- **Lark goes to look at the plants.** She tried to slip out of the conversation unnoticed and Billiam caught it, then let her go anyway.
- **Annie, in the yard.** Renna beans, coriander, mint, whatever fits in the window boxes, and a lot of sunlight this high up. Her family were among the first to move into the Stacks and she would not live anywhere else. Asked about Billiam's father, she said he is a professor at the College with a lot of important projects and is not home as often as they would like. **Insight: she is very sad about it, and hiding it.** Lark did not ask for surnames.
- **The night's leftovers, compared.** [[Theodore Blackwood|Theo]]'s house was searched by **Aeska in the morning and Felix in the afternoon**, and it had already been turned over before either of them, by somebody who had a key and locked up on the way out. Felix has until the end of the day to clear his tenement and decides to let them throw his things into the street.
- **Everyone stays.** Billiam asked his mother whether some of his friends could sleep over. **Another natural 20.** Aeska and Felix took her up on it. Lark, listening at the door, was caught bolting away from it by the whole room.

### Subterminal 8T

- **A sending, cut short.** Felix hears Harriet Spurnhold's voice in his head: *"Felix. This is really important. The Bellows. Subterminal 8T. Find it. Meet me there at midnight."* Rushed, and almost clipped off at the end.
- **Three of them go.** Felix knows nothing about the Bellows. **Lark and Calder do**, so they led. Aeska stayed behind rather than show his face to her; Billiam stayed because he does not like the Bellows.
- **The room.** Down through the residential tunnels near the chimney, where people live behind doors and where the [[The Below Boys|Below Boys]] work less often, then out into unfamiliar ground: a circular chamber with eight tunnels running off it and a single steam outlet in the middle venting up through grates to the street. Full of steam and curling smoke.
- **Lark saw her first**, took position on a crate against the wall, and held Calder back with her. Felix walked out into the room alone.
- **What Harriet said, before she was interrupted:**
  - She was sorry for the sending. It was an emergency.
  - **She locked Theo's office herself** after he died, to slow the guild's search down.
  - **She ransacked his house.** She went there the night he was killed, with a key he had given her long ago and had probably forgotten she still had, and made a mess of the place finding what she came for.
  - What she came for is a **ledger**: a log of **materials requisitioned from the Guild of Engineers and redirected out of their correct allocations** by Theo. She had been keeping the guild's own books straight to cover it.
  - She is fairly sure the guild knew she was dealing with Theo, and that this is why she was dismissed. She assumed Felix was dismissed for the same reason and had simply not been told what he was part of.
  - *"The really important thing -"*
- **She is shot in the throat.** A single shot out of the dark to the east, a magical burn across the neck, and she went down bleeding out mid-sentence with the ledger already in Felix's hands.
- **Calder ran at the shooter and found a dog.** Not the shooter: a **[[Constructs|howl]]**, and not a standard one. Modified, sleeker and more dangerous than the ones he had seen before, on its haunches ready to leap, and silent, because they do not growl. He panicked, missed it entirely, and ran.
- **Felix healed Harriet off the floor** and got her upright and moving on her own.
- **The shooter has an essence carbine.** A man leaning out over a barricade ten feet above the east side of the room, **overcharging** the weapon, two shots a turn. He put one into Felix. Lark caught it on a shield the size of a dinner plate that flared white on impact.
- **A second howl was whistled in.** Lark stood between the constructs and the others and knocked the first one flat twice.
- **The shooter runs.** Calder hit him with a chromatic orb and Felix with a ray of sickness that left him poisoned. Hurt, poisoned and well placed to leave, **he abandoned both dogs and went**, sixty feet a turn into the dark, with Calder unable to land a shot on him.
- **Lark went down**, mauled off her feet by the second howl, and was healed back up.
- **Lark burns.** She spent the fight transformed, freckles and eyes lit, radiant energy coming off her in a ten-foot circle that also caught Calder. The DM ruled it **keeps burning while she is unconscious**.
- **Felix went down**, and the howl **took the ledger in its mouth** and ran with it. His second death save was a **2**. A 1 would have killed him outright.
- **Lark ran it down.** Action surge, a sixty-foot dash after the fleeing construct, and she killed it on the last thing she had left. **The ledger came back with her.**
- **Calder stabilised Felix** with a medicine check as he was bleeding out, and Lark healed him back onto his feet.
- **They leave together**, four of them and the ledger, down a tunnel, with Harriet saying she does not know whether more of them are coming.

### Meanwhile, at the house

- **Aeska plays for Annie**, badgered into it by Billiam, and the performance is excellent despite the die.
- **[[Professor Benjamin Buckman]] comes home.** Keys in the door mid-song. He recognises Aeska on sight. The hug he gives Annie reads to both Billiam and Aeska as **two people who do not like each other**.
- **He speaks to Aeska without speaking.** He put out a hand to shake, Aeska bowed instead, and the extended hand became the gesture for a **message cantrip**: *"What are you doing here?"* Aeska answered that it was a long story, and that he meant neither his wife nor his son any harm. To Billiam it looked only like two men holding eye contact a beat too long.
- **He is not staying.** He said he had come to collect **research he had stored at the house**, and that he was very busy at the guild.
- **On the balcony, he tells Billiam to turn himself in.** The whole conversation, in order:
  - Forget you saw anything. Do not get involved. Go back to your life.
  - **Get rid of Aeska.** *"House Volan. He was touted to be the head of the Guild of Engineers when Dr. Tammerton passes on. He was kicked out. He's a wanted man."* His own family would like him **removed from the equation**, and he is a thorn in the engineers' side.
  - Aeska will use Billiam as bait and hand him to the guild as the culprit when it suits him.
  - **Turn yourself in to the Guild of Engineers.** Tell the truth, take a couple of years, maybe only months of hard labour. **Give them Aeska**, including where he is sleeping, and they may go easier on you.
  - *"They do not care whether you did it or not, but they will remove you from the board one way or another."*
- **Billiam asks him why he still works with them**, and gets through. **Insight, with advantage:** his father stumbles for a second before answering. The work is important. *"This is revolutionary."* He does not like the guild, but they are the only ones in a position to make it happen, and if he did not do it somebody else would, with a worse moral compass.
- **The thing Billiam is afraid of.** He said he barely holds a job because of what happens if he loses control, and referred back to an accident at the house. His father's answer was that **there are people inside the Guild of Engineers who can remove the ability**, that this is likely how they would deal with him if he became a problem, and that it is not what he wants for his son. *"There were people that would kill for the kind of power that you have, Billy."*
- **Benjamin has no pull at the guild.** He said so plainly. If it were the College holding Billiam, he would live in comfort. At the guild he can promise nothing.
- **Billiam agrees to send Aeska away.** He walked back inside to do it and **Aeska was gone.** Nobody at the table knows where.

**The session ends here.**

## NPCs, locations, factions

- [[Annie Buckman]] - Billiam's mother. Nurse at [[The Institute of Physikers]], born and raised in [[The Stacks]], from one of the first families to move up there. Warm, house-proud, feeds everyone. Quietly and carefully sad about how little her husband is home.
- [[Professor Benjamin Buckman]] - home for the first time in days, for one errand, and gone again. Knows Aeska on sight and by house. Knows enough about the Guild of Engineers to say what it does to inconvenient people. Has been working with them for years, on and off, on something he calls revolutionary and will not describe.
- [[Harriet Spurnhold]] - former Chief Secretary at the Guild of Engineers. Locked Theo's office to buy time and stripped his house herself. Shot in the throat handing over the ledger. Alive, healed twice, and out of the Bellows with the party.
- [[The scarred man|The scarred Aela man]] - a monk, and a professional. Step of the Wind, stunning strike, and a rope off his own belt. Says nothing at all, the whole fight, even when asked directly.
- [[The woman in green|The woman in the green coat]] *(Gertrude?)* - counterspelled Felix from thirty feet away without moving. Kept her distance the whole fight and was visibly uneasy about how many people were watching.
- **The man with the essence carbine** - unnamed, unseen except as a figure leaning over a barricade. Overcharged the weapon, two shots a turn, whistled the second howl in, and left the moment it stopped being cheap.
- **Two modified [[Constructs|howls]]** - hound constructs, silent, pneumatic jaws, built past the standard model. One of them was trained well enough to **pick the ledger up and carry it away**. Both destroyed.
- [[Dr. Mannsfield T. Tammerton|Dr. Tammerton]] - not present, but named as the man Aeska was raised to succeed, and as still alive.
- [[The Corewright's Association]] - the ones leaning on [[Claringbold's Yard]], not the Porter's Guild. Allied to the Guild of Engineers rather than answering to it, and generally does what it is told.
- [[The Porter's Guild]] - the two who attacked Lark wore the guild's own pins rather than crew pins. That is the level above the crews.
- [[Brassline]] - confirmed on tape as a Porter's Guild crew. Green coats from guild management did occasionally visit the depot.
- **Subterminal 8T**, [[The Bellows]] - a circular chamber with eight tunnels and a central steam vent, out past the residential tunnels near the chimney.

## Loot, contacts, consequences

- **The ledger.** Theo's private log of guild materials redirected out of their proper allocations. Technical names and numbers, some of it in a language Felix cannot read. **Lark is carrying it.**
- **Harriet Spurnhold is with the party**, alive, hunted, and the only person who can read that ledger.
- **Somebody is willing to shoot her in public to stop her handing it over**, and to send trained constructs specifically after whoever holds it.
- **Felix has no home as of tonight** and has decided not to go back for his things.
- **Aeska is missing**, and Billiam has just agreed to give him up.
- **Billiam's father knows** that his son was in Oblong Square, is being hunted, and is running with Aeska Volan.
- **Billiam has an ability he cannot control**, and now knows that the Guild of Engineers can take it away from him.
- **A fight in Oblong Square in front of a crowd**, in daylight, with the party on one side and two uniformed guild agents on the other.
