# Episode 3 - The Ledger

- **Date played:** 4 September 2026 / Tide 02, 756
- **In attendance:** Aeska, Billiam, Calder, Felix, Lark
- **In brief:** The fight in [[Oblong Square]] is survived, not won. The party goes to ground at Billiam's mother's house, where his father comes home for the first time in days and tells him to turn himself in. At midnight in the [[The Bellows|Bellows]], [[Harriet Spurnhold]] explains what she took from [[Theodore Blackwood|Theo]]'s house and is shot before she can finish the sentence.

## Key events

*One continuous evening, picking up where Episode 2 stopped, and splitting into
two scenes for the second half.*

### Oblong Square

- **The fight in the square.** The scarred man is a monk. He ran Lark down, stunned her and got a rope around her neck; she asked whether he would kill her for a name and he never answered, or spoke at all. Felix's first attempt to hold him was counterspelled by the woman in green.
- **The party pulled her out.** Calder shouted for the crowd and got it watching, which visibly worried the woman in green. Aeska's music took her out of the fight, Lark broke the grapple herself and ran, Felix's second Hold Person paralysed the monk, and Billiam spat at him in passing.
- **They got clear**, deeper into [[The Stacks]], with nobody following.

### On the run

- **The two who came for Lark wore [[The Porter's Guild]]'s own pins**, not crew pins, which puts them above the crews rather than in one. **Natural 20** on the politics check.
- **[[Brassline]] is a Porter's Guild crew**, so Lark quit the same guild the day before it came for her.
- **Lark gave up no names**, and said so to Aeska. [[Barry Killerman]] was left behind in the tavern as the only person seen talking to her.

### Billiam's house

- **Billiam took them home**, high in the Stacks, to a small three-room house built against a chimney for the warmth. He got his mother out of the room on a **natural 20**, and a bed for everyone who needed one on **another**.
- **[[Annie Buckman]]**, his mother, fed them and was delighted to have them. Asked about her husband, she gave the official version, professor at the College, important projects, not home as often as they would like. **Lark read her as very sad about it and hiding it.**
- **Aeska asked why the change of heart.** Billiam said he had not known who to trust and that it no longer mattered, because trouble was coming to them anyway. Aeska apologised for casting on him, and mentioned he has spent two years hiding.
- **Correction: the scrapyard visitors were the Corewrights.** The people leaning on [[Shrapnel]] at [[Claringbold's Yard]] and taxing him extra are **[[The Corewright's Association]]**, not the Porter's Guild.
- **Essence cores cost twenty to thirty ducats each**, per Billiam, who strips them for a living. Aeska added that [[The Guild of Engineers]] ran standing experiments on wiring many cores together. That matches [[Orson Thorne]]'s estimate of the weapon that killed Theo: a great deal of power, so a great many cores, so money and access.
- **Theo's house had been searched before either of them got there.** Aeska went in the morning and Felix in the afternoon, and somebody with a key had already turned it over and locked up again.
- **Felix let his belongings go.** Having lost his house, he had one day to collect them, and decided not to go back for them at all.

### Subterminal 8T

- **[[Harriet Spurnhold]] cast Sending to Felix**, sounding rushed and cutting off at the end: the Bellows, Subterminal 8T, midnight. Lark and Calder know the Bellows and took him down. Aeska and Billiam stayed at the house.
- **Felix went out to meet her** while Lark and Calder held back at the tunnel mouth. What she told him:
  - **She locked Theo's office** after he died, deliberately, to slow the guild's search.
  - **She ransacked his house** the night he was killed, with a key he had given her years before and probably forgotten she had.
  - What she took is a **ledger**: a log of **materials requisitioned from the Guild of Engineers and redirected out of their correct allocations** by Theo. She had been keeping the guild's books straight to cover it.
  - She believes the guild knew, and that this is why she was dismissed, and assumed Felix was dismissed for the same reason without ever being told what he was part of.
  - *"The really important thing -"*
- **She was shot in the neck mid-sentence**, from the dark, and went down bleeding out with the ledger already in Felix's hands.
  - Felix healed her back to consciousness and got her upright and moving on her own.
  - Calder went after the shooter and found a howl instead.
- **The fight.** A man on a barricade with an overcharged essence carbine, and two **modified [[Constructs|howls]]** past the standard model.
  - **The shooter left** once Calder and Felix had hurt and poisoned him, abandoning both constructs.
  - **Lark went down** under the second howl and was healed back up.
  - **Felix went down** and came within one point of a death save of dying outright. The howl **took the ledger out of his hands and ran with it**.
  - Lark chased the construct down, killed it and recovered the ledger. Calder stabilised Felix.
- **They left down a tunnel** with Harriet and the ledger.

### Meanwhile, at the house

- **[[Professor Benjamin Buckman]] came home** for the first time in days, while Aeska was playing for Annie. He recognised Aeska on sight, and **spoke to him by message cantrip** using an offered handshake as cover. The hug he gave Annie read to both Billiam and Aeska as two people who do not like each other.
- **He was not staying.** He had come to collect research he keeps stored at the house.
- **On the balcony he told Billiam to turn himself in** to the Guild of Engineers, tell the truth, and take a couple of years. *"They do not care whether you did it or not, but they will remove you from the board one way or another."*
- **And to give up Aeska.** *"House Volan. He was touted to be the head of the Guild of Engineers when Dr. Tammerton passes on. He was kicked out. He's a wanted man."* His own family would like him removed from the equation. Handing over where he sleeps might buy leniency.
- **Billiam asked why he still works with them** and caught his father stumbling before he answered. The work is important, *"this is revolutionary"*, and if he did not do it somebody else would with a worse moral compass.
- **Billiam's ability came up.** He said he barely holds a job because of what happens if he loses control. His father's answer was that **there are people inside the Guild of Engineers who can remove the ability**, that it is how they would deal with him if he became a problem, and that it is not what he wants for him.
- **Billiam agreed to send Aeska away**, walked back inside to do it, and **Aeska had gone.**

**The session ends here.**

## NPCs, locations, factions

- [[Annie Buckman]] - Billiam's mother. Warm and house-proud, born and raised in [[The Stacks]]. Sad about how little her husband is home, and hiding it.
- [[Professor Benjamin Buckman]] - home for one errand and gone again. Knows Aeska on sight and by house. Has worked with the Guild of Engineers for years on something he calls revolutionary and will not describe.
- [[Harriet Spurnhold]] - locked Theo's office to buy time and stripped his house herself, for the ledger. Shot handing it over. Alive, and with the party.
- [[The scarred man|The scarred Aela man]] - a monk. Did not speak once during the fight, including when Lark asked him a direct question.
- [[The woman in green|The woman in the green coat]] *(Gertrude?)* - counterspelled Felix at thirty feet, then spent most of the fight incapacitated. Uneasy about the size of the crowd.
- **The man with the essence carbine** - unnamed. Overcharged the weapon, whistled in a second howl, and left once he was hurt and poisoned.
- **Two modified [[Constructs|howls]]** - past the standard model. One was trained well enough to **take the ledger and carry it away**. Both destroyed.
- [[Dr. Mannsfield T. Tammerton|Dr. Tammerton]] - not present. Named as still alive and still in post.
- [[The Corewright's Association]] - the ones leaning on [[Claringbold's Yard]].
- [[The Porter's Guild]] - the two who attacked Lark wore the guild's own pins, not crew pins.
- [[Brassline]] - confirmed as a Porter's Guild crew.
- **Subterminal 8T**, [[The Bellows]] - a circular chamber with eight tunnels and a central steam vent, out past the residential tunnels.

## Loot, contacts, consequences

- **The ledger.** Theo's log of guild materials redirected out of their proper allocations. Technical names and numbers, some in a language Felix cannot read. **Lark is carrying it.**
- **Harriet Spurnhold is with the party**, alive and hunted. She is the one who kept the guild's books to cover what is in the ledger.
- **Somebody will shoot her in public to stop her handing it over**, and send trained constructs after whoever holds it.
- **Felix has no home** and nothing but what he is carrying.
- **Aeska is missing**, and Billiam has agreed to give him up.
- **Billiam's father knows** his son was in Oblong Square, is being hunted, and is running with Aeska Volan.
- **A fight in Oblong Square in front of a crowd**, against two uniformed guild agents.
