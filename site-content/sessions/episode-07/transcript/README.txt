EPISODE 7 - RAW TRANSCRIPT
==========================

Played 4 October 2026. Three hours forty-nine minutes.

WhisperX output from the session recording, with speaker diarisation.

FILES
-----
  episode-07.txt      the readable transcript, one line per utterance,
                      prefixed with the speaker tag. Start here.
  episode-07.srt      the same content with timestamps, so it can be played
                      alongside the audio in any media player
  episode-07.vtt      the same again in WebVTT, for browser players
  episode-07.tsv      start/end milliseconds and text, for scripting
  episode-07.json     word-level timings. Large, and only useful if you are
                      doing something programmatic with it.
  speaker_map.json    which SPEAKER_NN tag belongs to which person

Source material. None of this is published to the website: the site builder
only reads .md files, so everything in this folder lives in the repository
only. The word-level .json, the .vtt and the .tsv are deliberately not
committed; the .json alone is over 6 MB.

THREE THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE YOU TRUST IT
----------------------------------------
1. ONE LINE IS REDACTED. A player was referred to by their real name once,
   out of character, and that now reads "[Lark]". Nothing else has been
   altered.

2. TWO TAGS ARE BINS. SPEAKER_01 and SPEAKER_04 are both mapped to UNCERTAIN
   because neither is one person: between them they collect short
   interjections from several speakers. The other six tags are reliable.

   WhisperX mangles proper nouns throughout. In this episode: "Colder",
   "Kolder", "Kaldor", "Golda", "Carla", "Cody", "Colin" and "Kestrel" are
   Calder; "Lakk", "Laak", "Larg" and "Larka" are Lark; "Aski", "Aiskar" and
   "Ayaska" are Aeska; "Biliam", "Bili", "Biddy" and "Dilliam" are Billiam;
   "Krenzler", "Grimton" and "Krimz" are Krimson; "Delilah" is Delia;
   "Stalder" and "Salvador" are Salador's Slip; "Shrutnel" is Shrapnel;
   "Bertrude" is Gertrude; "Alastair" is Alistar; "Berkman" is Buckman.

   One to watch: "Kimmy died" near the end is table banter, and the GM
   corrects it in the next line. Nobody died permanently.

3. THE SPEAKER MAP IS PER-EPISODE. WhisperX assigns SPEAKER_00, SPEAKER_01 and
   so on in the order it happens to find voices, so the numbering is different
   in every episode and carries no meaning across them. Always read the
   speaker_map.json sitting next to the transcript you are using.

The transcript is the only source of truth for what happened in a session.
That still holds. Just read it knowing the labels are approximate even where
the words are not.
