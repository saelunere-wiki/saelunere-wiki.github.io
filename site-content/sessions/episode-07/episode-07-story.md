### A note on how this is written

It follows what actually happened at the table, and the events, the order of them
and the outcomes are all accurate. But it is written as a story rather than as a
record, so some moments have been smoothed, some sentences and quotes have been
tidied or added, and characters are sometimes given a feeling in a place where
the table moved straight on.

If you read your own character doing or saying something that is **horrendously
wrong**, or that you would simply rather was not there, tell me and I will change
it. That goes for small things too.
