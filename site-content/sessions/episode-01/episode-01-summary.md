# Episode 1 - Harvest Fest

- **Date played:** 7 August 2026 / Terr 00, 756
- **In attendance:** Aeska, Billiam, Calder, Felix, Lark
- **In brief:** [[Theodore Blackwood]] is shot dead in front of a crowd while announcing a campaign for an elected [[Parvo]], and the five who did not run are left standing over his body when the [[The Guild of Engineers|Guild of Engineers]] arrives.

## Key events

- **Harvest Fest at Oblong Square.** All five were in the crowd for the festival without knowing each other. Aeska was playing the corner stage at [[The Plumb and Bob]], Calder and Lark were drinking in it, Billiam was outside on a stack of crates, and Felix arrived escorting Theo, whose secretary he was.
- **The taverns emptied for the speech.** [[Harl Mott]] closed the Plumb and Bob and pushed everyone out to listen, as did every other landlord on [[Oblong Square]].
- **Theo's proposal.** He allowed that the guilds had done great things when the fate of humanity rested on them, but that this was thirty years ago, and put forward a new idea: **a vote, for the leadership and the future of Parvo, in which every Parvozian gets a say.**
- **He was shot mid-sentence.** A single shot opened his chest and dropped him off the back of the stage. The crowd scattered. Four of the five went towards him instead, and Aeska held back in a side street.
- **He was dead before anyone reached him.** No healing would take. The wound was far beyond a pistol or an essence crossbow, and from the way he fell, the shot came from the direction of [[The Core|the Core]], away from [[The Stacks]]. Looking that way, Lark counted rooftops and **four guild spires** with clean sightlines onto the stage.
- **Aeska called them off the body**, warning that the guards would not care who fired the shot, only who was standing over the corpse. Billiam refused, so Aeska cast **Charm Person** on him. Billiam shrugged it off, felt the magic, and has distrusted him since. Lark ended up grappling him away.
- **The enforcers arrived.** Two guardsmen and an officer in the **black and gold** of the [[The Guild of Engineers|Guild of Engineers]], within minutes. Aeska bolted for the Plumb and Bob's back door before they arrived.
- **Felix pulled rank** and claimed the other three, on the strength of outranking guardsmen from an unrelated department. The officer was unimpressed, offered him a private way out, and made clear he was taking the other three in as witnesses. Felix refused.
- **The officer was not surprised.** Billiam noticed that when told a man had been shot, he reacted as though he already knew. Meanwhile the guardsmen dragged Theo's body across the cobbles.
- **[[Sergeant Tanner]] named himself**, and then took psychic damage from no visible source, grabbed his head and ran. **Nobody in the party cast it.**
- **The fight.** Tanner assumed the fleeing Billiam was responsible and shot him twice with an essence carbine. Lark knocked one guardsman out cold in a single blow. Calder killed Tanner with **Chromatic Orb**. The last guardsman fled the square, having seen all their faces.
- **Then someone burned the survivor.** The unconscious guardsman's breastplate began to glow red while the party stood over it. Calder identified **Heat Metal**, which needs line of sight. At least twenty windows overlook the centre of the square. They could not get the armour off him.
- **They went to ground at the Plumb and Bob.** Aeska paid Harl **10 ducats** to have never seen them. Harl agreed happily, being no friend of the guilds, and offered to tell anyone asking that it was [[The Below Boys]].
- **Introductions.** Felix had been Theo's secretary about a year. Calder had known him four or five years and credits him with opening doors. Lark admired him and says he was there when she needed someone. For Billiam he was an acquaintance of his father who got him a job. Only Felix is guild.
- **Aeska explained himself.** He gave his name as **Eska Volan**, son of the Volan family, his father second in command to [[Dr. Tammerton]] and himself raised to succeed Tammerton, until he was **disowned**. Theo was a friend during his guild years, though they had not spoken in two. His read: only a handful of guards were sent where there should have been an army, and the officer showed no interest in the dead man. He thinks it was planned.
- **The arrangement.** Everyone works their own contacts and meets again at the Plumb and Bob on **Wex Day**, three days on.

## NPCs, locations, factions

- [[Theodore Blackwood]] - **killed**, shot on stage in front of a crowd who knew him, then dragged off the square by guild enforcers.
- [[Sergeant Tanner]] - Guild of Engineers Guardsmen Brigade. Wanted three witnesses taken to the spire, was incurious about the dead man, and was killed after opening fire on Billiam.
- [[Harl Mott]] - barman at the Plumb and Bob. No friend of the guilds, and bought for 10 ducats.
- [[The Guild of Engineers]] - Theo was its **Chief Essence Transfer Supervisor**. Its **Guardsmen Brigade** wear black and gold.
- [[Dr. Tammerton]] - not present. Named as the man Aeska's father serves, and the man Aeska was raised to succeed.
- [[Oblong Square]] - overlooked by four guild spires and some twenty windows, with sightlines onto the stage clean enough to be alarming in hindsight.
- [[The Below Boys]] - not involved, but Harl intends to blame them.

## Loot, contacts, consequences

- **One guardsman escaped**, and saw all of their faces.
- **Two enforcers dead and one left burning** in the square, one of them killed by Calder.
- **Aeska is 10 ducats down**, and Harl Mott is bought.
- **Aeska's family and disowning are now known** to the other four.
- Everyone agreed to work their contacts. Felix agreed outright to help him.
