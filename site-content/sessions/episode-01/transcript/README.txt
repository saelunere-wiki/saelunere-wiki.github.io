EPISODE 1 - RAW TRANSCRIPT
==========================

WhisperX output from the session recording, with speaker diarisation.
Source material. None of this is published to the website: the site builder
only reads .md files, so everything in this folder lives in the repository
only. It is here so that players and the DM can work from the same source
instead of passing files around.

FILES
-----
  episode-01.txt      the readable transcript, one line per utterance,
                      prefixed with the speaker tag
  episode-01.srt      the same content with timestamps, so it can be played
                      alongside the audio in any media player
  speaker_map.json    which SPEAKER_NN tag belongs to which person

The word-level .json and the .vtt are deliberately not committed. The .json
is roughly 6 MB per episode and the .vtt duplicates the .srt.

TWO THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE YOU TRUST IT
--------------------------------------
1. REAL NAMES HAVE BEEN REPLACED. Where someone said a player's real name
   out of character, it has been replaced with that player's CHARACTER name
   in square brackets, e.g. "[Aeska]", "[Billy]", "[Felix]". Four lines are
   affected. Nothing else has been altered.

2. THE DIARISATION IS IMPERFECT. The speaker tags are mostly right but not
   always, and the opening round of introductions in particular has lines
   attributed to the wrong person. WhisperX also mangles proper nouns
   throughout: "Asuka" and "Asker" are Aeska, "Harle" and "Hall" are Harl,
   "Plum and Bob" is the Plumb and Bob, "Blowboys" are the Below Boys, and
   Arcanography appears as "archonography" and "iconography". Check names
   against the wiki rather than copying them out of here.

Per the Canon & Discipline rules, the transcript is the only source of truth
for what happened in a session. That still holds. Just read it knowing the
labels are approximate even where the words are not.
