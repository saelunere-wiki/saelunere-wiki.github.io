# Episode 6 - What Is In His Chest

- **Date played:** 25 September 2026 / Tide 04 into Tide 05, 756
- **In attendance:** Aeska, Billiam, Calder, Felix, Lark
- **In brief:** Getting out of [[The Head of Steam]] costs Billiam his anonymity. Back at the yard, Felix tells the party what his parents did to him four years ago, and a hidden safe turns out to have been emptied the same day. The next afternoon a delivery arrives that is not a delivery.

## Key events

*Straight on from the end of Episode 5, then the following day.*

### Getting out of the Head of Steam

- **The masked man walked through the wall.** He took his leave of Evan, crossed to the window Billiam was clinging to, and **walked out through the brick and glass two storeys up**, staying level in the air. He looked left and found Billiam a foot away. They were equally surprised.
- **Billiam cast Sleep on him and it did nothing.** A wild magic surge made it a bonus action. The man waved the spell off, jumped, and was gone. **He has seen Billiam's face.**
- **Lark saw it from the alley** and describes a ghost: a white face and a black cloak that appeared out of a wall and vanished.
- **Billiam wears a bracelet** that pulls his wild magic back into his arm so it does not show.
- **Felix stayed behind as a scratcher and watched the room empty.** Evan left. Then the woman in green, **whose name Felix now learns is [[The woman in green|Gertrude]]**, went to the scarred Aela man, who had been beaten badly.
  - **She called him Alistair, and called him son.** *"Alistair, I'm sorry. I'm sorry about this, son."* She wiped the blood off his face and walked him out through the bar, telling the staff he had had a few too many.
  - **He is far more hurt than he should be.** A few punches from a nobleman, against a man who took Shrapnel apart without difficulty.
- **Felix left by the floor**, found Aeska's boots in the crowd and scratched his ankle. Lark popped his head open looking for a message, worked out what he was, and carried him out inside her jacket.

### Back at Claringbold's Yard

- **Felix changed back in front of everybody**, on the office table: legs lengthening into limbs with a noise of gears being pulled apart, leaving him curled on the floor with all his clothes still on. Lark got a static shock off his arm.
- **Then he explained himself, and it is the largest thing anybody has learned yet:**
  - **He was born a noma**, one of the uncommon people who cannot use magic at all no matter what they try. Billiam's mother is one.
  - **Four years ago his parents took him to a warehouse south of the city and handed him over.** He was held **three years**. He came out with **a device in place of his heart**, and that is where his magic comes from.
  - He thinks they did it so that he would have magic. Theo knew, and told him his parents were gone.
  - **Nothing like this is known to exist.** Aeska's engineering and Billiam's College training agree: transformation magic is ordinary, granting people magical ability is ordinary, but **fusing essence machinery into a living body is not something anyone has done or even theorised.** Prosthetics are unpowered for exactly this reason.
- **Shrapnel had a hidden safe**, cut into the brick of the back office behind a painting. **It is open and empty.**
  - It was **cut apart with a heavy welding torch over several hours** by somebody who knew it was there but did not have the code.
  - **It happened that day**, while they were out. Only [[Alfie]] and [[Hollis]] were at the yard.
- **Aeska found the employee records**, which give him names and addresses for both men. He failed once more to repair Shepherd.

### Lark and Madame Rooke

- **She paid her debt with Evan Langford instead of Aeska**: the cores, the trade, and who is running it.
- **[[Madame Rooke]] called it settled**, and more than she had expected in a day. She will not sell it to the Engineers or the Porters, who would be unhappy she knows at all, but other guilds pay well for word of new core technology.
- **She will stop looking for Aeska.** Her warning: she is one route to information and not the only one, rumours are hard to stamp out, and Lark should pass that on.
- **Lark pitched herself as ears in the bathhouse.** Rooke told her she was getting ahead of herself, they already know what happens under their own roof, but offered her jobs and told her to come back tomorrow.

### The next day

- **Calder** woke at his parents' house and warned [[Johan Flynn|Johan]] and [[Vanessa Flynn|Vanessa]] that somebody might come looking. His father picked up a wrench and offered to wallop them. They would rather he did not bring this home.
- **Felix** started work at [[Fexfield Scrappers]], greeted at the door by **[[Hugh Fexfield|Hugh]]** and set by [[Lucile Fexfield|Lucile]] on a cupboard of unlabelled binders nobody has dealt with in years.
- **The others regrouped at the yard** and argued about what to do: confront Alfie and Hollis over the safe, work the name Evan, or go back to the Head of Steam, where Aeska is now welcome.

### The delivery

- **The gate bell rang.** An older man in overalls with a cart and a large crate, driven by a young Aela with a dust cloth over his face. Billiam did not recognise either of them, and deliveries are normally arranged in advance.
- **He reached inside his coat as if for paperwork and pulled out a cudgel**, swinging at Billiam's head. It was meant to put him down in one.
- **Billiam answered with Thunderwave**, catching both men and the cart and throwing them ten feet across the cobbles. Both went down, and the cart broke apart.
- **Something got out of the crate.** An **eight-foot creature, part flesh and part machine**, one arm dragging, with a **greenish essence core embedded in its chest**. It took the blast, stood up, and charged him.
- **Nobody else has seen it yet.** The session ends there.

## NPCs, locations, factions

- **The man in the porcelain mask** - walks through walls and stands in mid-air. Shrugged off a Sleep spell without apparent effort. **Has seen Billiam's face.**
- [[The woman in green|Gertrude]] - named at last, in front of Felix. Tends the scarred man's injuries herself and calls him **son**.
- **Alistair** - the scarred Aela monk, named for the first time. Beaten badly by someone at that table, and taken home.
- [[Evan Langford]] - left the room before any of it. Aeska is welcome back to play for him.
- [[Alfie]] and [[Hollis]] - the only two at the yard the day the safe was emptied.
- [[Hugh Fexfield|Hugh]] and [[Lucile Fexfield|Lucile]] - Felix's new employers. He is inside their paperwork from today.
- **The creature in the crate** - flesh and machine, core in the chest, delivered to a scrapyard in a box.

## Loot, contacts, consequences

- **Felix's magic comes from a device where his heart should be**, and the whole party now knows it.
- **The masked man can put a face to Billiam.**
- **Lark's debt to Madame Rooke is paid**, she is out of the search for Aeska, and Lark has work there.
- **Aeska is welcome at the Head of Steam** as a performer.
- **Two men are lying in the street outside Claringbold's**, unconscious or dead.
- **Shrapnel's safe is empty** and whatever was in it is gone.
