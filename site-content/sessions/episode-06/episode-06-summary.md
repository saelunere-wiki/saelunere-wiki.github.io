# Episode 6 - What Is In His Chest

- **Date played:** 25 September 2026 / Tide 04 into Tide 05, 756
- **In attendance:** Aeska, Billiam, Calder, Felix, Lark
- **In brief:** Getting out of [[The Head of Steam]] costs Billiam his anonymity. Back at the yard, Felix tells the party what his parents did to him four years ago, and a hidden safe turns out to have been emptied the same day. The next afternoon a delivery arrives that is not a delivery.

## Key events

*Straight on from the end of Episode 5, then the following day.*

### Getting out of the Head of Steam

- **The masked man walked out through the wall**, two storeys up, and found Billiam on the ledge. Billiam cast Sleep; he waved it off and vanished. **He has seen Billiam's face.**
- **Lark saw it from the alley** and took Billiam out.
- **Billiam wears a bracelet** that absorbs his wild magic so a surge does not show.
- **Felix stayed as a scratcher and watched the room empty.** Evan left. The woman in green, **whose name Felix now learns is [[The woman in green|Gertrude]]**, tended the scarred Aela man and walked him out past the staff as a drunk.
  - **She called him Alistair, and called him son.** *"Alistair, I'm sorry. I'm sorry about this, son."*
  - **He had been badly beaten** - far worse than a few punches from a nobleman should leave the man who took Shrapnel apart.
- **Lark worked out what the scratcher was** and carried him out inside her jacket.

### Back at Claringbold's Yard

- **Felix changed back in front of everybody.** Lark got a static shock off his arm.
- **What he told them:**
  - **He was born a noma**, one of the uncommon people who cannot use magic at all. Billiam's mother is one.
  - **Four years ago his parents took him to a warehouse south of the city and handed him over.** He was held **three years** and came out with **a device in place of his heart**. That is where his magic comes from.
  - He believes they did it so he would have magic. **Theo knew**, and told him his parents were gone.
  - **Nothing like it is known to exist.** Transformation and granted ability are both ordinary, but **fusing essence machinery into a living body has never been done or theorised.** It is why prosthetics are unpowered.
- **Shrapnel had a hidden safe**, cut into the brick behind a painting. **Open and empty.**
  - **Cut apart with a welding torch over several hours**, by somebody who knew it was there but not the code, **the same day** while they were out. Only [[Alfie]] and [[Hollis]] were at the yard.
- **Aeska found the employee records**: names and addresses for both men.

### Lark and Madame Rooke

- **She paid the debt with Evan Langford instead of Aeska** - the cores, the trade, and who runs it. **[[Madame Rooke]] called it settled.**
- **She will stop looking for Aeska**, with the warning that she is one route of many and rumours are hard to stamp out.
- **She will not sell it to the Engineers or the Porters**, who would be unhappy she knows, but other guilds pay for word of new core technology.
- **Lark now works for her**, with jobs to come.

### The next day

- **Calder** warned [[Johan Flynn|Johan]] and [[Vanessa Flynn|Vanessa]] that somebody might come looking.
- **Felix** started at [[Fexfield Scrappers]], set by [[Lucile Fexfield|Lucile]] on years of unsorted paperwork.
- **The others weighed three leads**: Alfie and Hollis over the safe, the name Evan, or the Head of Steam.

### The delivery

- **A cart arrived at the gate** with a large crate. Neither the carter nor the driver was a regular, and the delivery had not been arranged.
- **The carter pulled a cudgel** and swung at Billiam's head, meaning to put him down in one.
- **Billiam answered with Thunderwave**, throwing both men and the cart across the cobbles. Both went down; the crate broke open.
- **Something got out of it.** An **eight-foot creature, part flesh and part machine**, with a **greenish essence core embedded in its chest**. It took the blast and charged him.
- **Nobody else has seen it yet.** The session ends there.

## NPCs, locations, factions

- **The man in the porcelain mask** - walks through walls and stands in mid-air. Shrugged off a Sleep spell without apparent effort. **Has seen Billiam's face.**
- [[The woman in green|Gertrude]] - named at last, in front of Felix. Tends the scarred man's injuries herself and calls him **son**.
- **Alistair** - the scarred Aela monk, named for the first time. Beaten badly by someone at that table, and taken home.
- [[Evan Langford]] - left the room before any of it. Aeska is welcome back to play for him.
- [[Alfie]] and [[Hollis]] - the only two at the yard the day the safe was emptied.
- [[Hugh Fexfield|Hugh]] and [[Lucile Fexfield|Lucile]] - Felix's new employers. He is inside their paperwork from today.
- **The creature in the crate** - flesh and machine, core in the chest, delivered to a scrapyard in a box.

## Loot, contacts, consequences

- **Felix's magic comes from a device where his heart should be**, and the whole party now knows it.
- **The masked man can put a face to Billiam.**
- **Lark's debt to Madame Rooke is paid**, she is out of the search for Aeska, and Lark has work there.
- **Aeska is welcome at the Head of Steam** as a performer.
- **Two men are lying in the street outside Claringbold's**, unconscious or dead.
- **Shrapnel's safe is empty** and whatever was in it is gone.
