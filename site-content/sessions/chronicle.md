# The Chronicle

The days of the campaign in order, from the morning of Harvest Fest onward. One
or two lines each: what the day was, not what it meant. The episode pages are
where the detail lives.

This is a **living record**. The most recent day is usually still being played,
and its line is rewritten once the next session finishes it.
