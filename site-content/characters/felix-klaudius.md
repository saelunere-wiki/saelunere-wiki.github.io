# Felix Klaudius

## At a glance

- **Race:** [[Darkin]]
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- Formerly secretary to [[Theodore Blackwood]] at [[The Guild of Engineers]].

## Appearance

Male Darkin: dark slate-grey/blue skin, a pair of large ridged tan horns curving up and back from the brow. Long deep-purple hair swept back with loose strands framing the face; pointed elf-like ears; bright green eyes; dark goatee and mustache (a few purple strands). Wears a rust/reddish-brown collared coat. Carries himself as dexterous and hard to track.

## Identity

He was Theo's secretary for a year, with an office directly alongside his. He describes the relationship as **mostly work**. He knows his own maintenance department very well and little of the guild beyond it.

> *The party can see he is not lying, and can also see there is more to it than
> he is saying.*

## History

He was escorting Theo to the stage at Harvest Fest, carrying his things, and was beside him when he was shot. When Guild of Engineers guardsmen arrived he declared himself a member of the guild and pulled rank on them, was offered a quiet way out on his own, and refused it rather than leave the others.

**In two days he lost everything.** On the first morning he arrived at the spire to find Theo's floor full of clerks and guardsmen he did not recognise, there to search a dead man's belongings for guild secrets. Chief Secretary [[Harriet Spurnhold]] had locked the office and was holding them off. Felix had a key, let them in, searched alongside them, and steered them away from anything that mattered. There was nothing incriminating to find. By the end of that day he had been offered, and had accepted, **Theo's own job on an interim basis**.

The next morning there was a letter under his door. **Terminated from the guild, and evicted from his guild tenement by the end of the day.** He was turned away at the spire doors, and his request to speak to Spurnhold came back that she was no longer there either.

He then went up into [[The Stacks]] to Theo's house, intending to force the lock, and found the door already broken open and the place ransacked. An elderly neighbour came to the door looking for Theo, and he passed himself off as the secretary. She told him **the house had been searched the night Theo died** and that Theo never came home. He pushed past her on the way out, and the street got a look at his face.
