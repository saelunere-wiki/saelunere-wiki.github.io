# Billiam Buckman

## At a glance

- **Race:** [[Darkin]]
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- Goes by Billy. Works the recycling stations at [[Claringbold's Yard]].

## Appearance

Young Darkin, light lavender-purple skin, short dark hair, curved ridged grey horns sweeping back, pointed ears, an almost-permanent wide warm grin. Green hooded cloak over a tan/brown leather jerkin and lighter shirt, crossbody satchel and belt pouch.

## Identity

Everybody calls him **Billy**. Billiam is for his parents and for official paperwork.

He is the son of [[Professor Benjamin Buckman]] and [[Annie Buckman]]. He told the party he was once Buckman's apprentice at [[The College of Arcanographers]] and let it stand there, until Episode 4, when he told Calder that Buckman is his father.

## History

He was an apprentice under Professor Buckman at the College a few years ago, and left because it was not for him: he does not much like books and is not a strong reader. What he studied there was magic and essence. [[Theodore Blackwood]], who knew his father, is the one who found him his job at the scrapyard afterwards.

**He has told the party plainly that he will not fight, and will not use magic.** His words: people die because of magic, and people get hurt because of magic, and he does not want to be part of that. He turned down an offer from Calder to teach him non-lethal self-defence, and asked [[Aeska Volan|Aeska]] never to cast on him again, which Aeska agreed to.

He says he is here because he wants to know whether corruption is spreading through the guilds.

## Connections

- [[Theodore Blackwood]] - an acquaintance of his father who got him his job. See [[Episode 1 - Summary]].
- [[Professor Benjamin Buckman]] - his father, and formerly his master at the College.
- [[Annie Buckman]] - his mother, on call at [[The Institute of Physikers]] the night Theo died.
- [[Shrapnel]] - his boss at [[Claringbold's Yard]].
- [[Calder Flynn]] - holds the College place Billy left behind, which the two of them worked out in conversation.

## Episode 1

He watched the speech from outside [[The Plumb and Bob]], up on a stack of crates. When Theo fell he went towards him with three of the others, and when Aeska told them to leave the body **he refused**. Aeska cast Charm Person on him; he shrugged it off and felt the magic, and after that he was openly suspicious of Aeska. He told Lark he did not know what he was doing, only that he was trying to protect Theo's body, and it was Lark who finally grappled him away.

When the Guild of Engineers officer was told that a man had been shot, **Billiam noticed that he reacted as though he already knew.** In the fight that followed, Sergeant Tanner took Billiam for the culprit as he ran and **shot him twice in the back** with an essence carbine. He hid behind crates in the alley, scared, holding a small dagger in both hands, until [[Calder Flynn|Calder]] saw him panicking, held him up and walked him to the Plumb and Bob. Healed there, he thanked Felix.

When Aeska started questioning everyone, Billiam refused to answer: *"you haven't even told us your name and you start investigating all of us while you're the most suspicious guy here."* And: *"as far as I see it, you ran away from us."* Afterwards [[Lark]] came and sat by him: she had seen he did not want to leave Theo, and had felt the same. *"Seems like you knew the best of him, like I did."* He told her to call him Billy.

## Episode 2

At [[Claringbold's Yard]] he told [[Shrapnel]] about the killing and found him unmoved: the guilds mess with everyone's business, and of course they collected their own dead. He also told Shrapnel that something bad was going on, with the men who had been coming to the yard that week, and Shrapnel talked past it.

From his mother he learned that **the Institute was on call that night and the nurses waited for Theo's body to be brought in for healing, and it never arrived.** She hugged him, and he kept the wince from his wounds to himself. His father has not been home for days, off-site on some project, and Billiam told his mother he wished his father were home more. When Billiam went to the College to find him he was turned away at the door, and the secretary told him she had heard of his short tenure there: he had been a disappointment then and was one now. He left sad, though not before telling her that at least he did not forget his lunchbox.

In the back room he was drawn to Lark more than to anyone else there, and did not yet trust the others, Aeska least of all. When Calder named Professor Buckman as the man who got him his place, Billiam said **he had been Buckman's apprentice before Calder**, and left because College was not for him: *"I'm just not that smart, maybe."* He did not say that Buckman is his father. He asked Aeska never to cast on him again, and told the group he is not a fighter and does not want to be one. When the Porter's agents came, Lark covered for him outside, calling him her little brother and pushing him clear.

## Episode 3

He came out of the tavern by the front door, arrived in [[Oblong Square]] at a
sprint, passed the scarred man at the moment he was frozen and unable to move, and
**spat in his face** on the way by.

Afterwards he **took the party home** - up the ladders and ramps of
[[The Stacks]] to the small three-room house he grew up in, built flush against a
chimney for the warmth. Asked why the sudden change of heart after two days of
telling them nothing, he said he did not know who to trust, and that it no longer
matters, because trouble is coming to them anyway. He put
[[Aeska Volan|Aeska]] and [[Felix Klaudius|Felix]] up for the night. Asked whether
he would go to the Bellows with Felix, he said he did not like the Bellows much.

He strips essence cores for a living and priced them for the others at **twenty to
thirty ducats each**, sometimes more, which is where the party's arithmetic about
the murder weapon started.

Then his father came home. Billiam introduced Aeska to him as *"one of my new
friends... friends might not be the word."* On the balcony he asked his father for
help: *"I'm in big trouble and I need your help."* Told to give himself up to
[[The Guild of Engineers]] and to hand Aeska over, he defended Aeska: *"I am not
saying I fully trust that guy, but he has helped us get away with our lives."* He
asked what happens if he goes to prison and has another accident, like the floor at
home. His father told him there are people in the Guild of Engineers who can remove
the ability. *"I have to live on my toes everywhere I go,"* Billiam said. *"I barely
have a job because of what might happen if I lose control."* His father: *"It's not
just a curse, it's a gift."*

He did not want to tell Aeska to go himself, and asked his father to do it. When his
father refused, **he told his father he would send Aeska away**: *"Dad, I trust you.
If you think that that is what's best, I will do it."* He walked back inside to do it
and Aeska was already gone.

## Episode 4

On Wexdae he spent the walk to work turning over what his father and Aeska had to do
with each other, and found [[Claringbold's Yard]] silent: the gate unlatched, nobody
at work, [[Shrapnel]] gone and the office smashed. Both of the yard's Bullyboys had
been attacked. He told them to hang in there, and was angry: someone had hurt them on
purpose. He spent the morning failing to repair [[Shepherd]] and [[Steam Bun]].

When the others arrived he recognised a serial number from Theo's ledger: the
unfamiliar machine he and Shrapnel took apart weeks ago, whose **fifteen cores
Shrapnel called more powerful than normal** and took away himself. From what
Shrapnel said, it was the second machine of its type to come in. He also knows
[[Fexfield Scrappers]], the other name in the ledger, as a competitor.

He told Aeska that his father had told him to stay away from Aeska and to turn
himself in to the Guild of Engineers, and that he had said he would and is not
going to. He had been afraid Aeska overheard and took it the wrong way. His father
thinks prison is better than him getting killed, *"which I guess it's nice that he
cares."* He has been through his father's things and **found something marked
with a V**, and it was recent. He asked Lark whether the Brassline is a big crew,
and whether its couriers ever go inside people's houses when they deliver. When Lark
wondered whether Calder was against them, he said he did not think so. Of Lark's
idea that Aeska take Theo's place: *"I don't see Aeska as a great friend, but I
wouldn't want him to get shot in a public setting."* And he told
[[Calder Flynn|Calder]] that Professor Buckman is his father: when he said he was
Buckman's apprentice he was not fully honest, *"but I didn't lie."*

*"I would really want to talk to Shrapnel and ask him what happened."* He plans to
check the yard at nine the next morning and meet Felix at Fexfield Scrappers at ten.

## Episode 5

At [[Claringbold's Yard]] he met [[Alfie]] and [[Hollis]], back only for their
wages. Shrapnel had sent the crew away when the agents turned up; Hollis does not expect to
see him again, and said he might be dead. Billiam would not have it, and said he
would stay and find out what had happened to Shrapnel. He let them into the office to
take what they were owed. **The yard is finished**, and he suggested it as the
party's new meeting place.

At [[Fexfield Scrappers]] he told [[Lucile Fexfield]] he had worked at Claringbold's
and at the Guild of Engineers, asked one question too many about guilds, and was
passed over for anything but scrapping work. He told her Felix was a good guy who
would fit in. When he admitted why they had come, she told them about Shrapnel's
contracts off the books and the Porter's Guild as buyers. He told her he did not want
to give up on Shrapnel; she told him to take care of Shrapnel, and he said he would
try.

At [[The Head of Steam]] he climbed the pipework in the alley to look in at the
upstairs windows. To get past the fourth, he threw a phantom mumble of voices by
its door so the room looked the other way. He watched two porters leave that room,
and got a line of sight into the fifth.

## Episode 6

On the ledge outside room five, **a man in a porcelain mask walked out through the
wall** beside him, two storeys up. In a panic Billiam cast Sleep on him; it did
nothing, and the man turned invisible and was gone. They were a foot apart: **he has
seen Billiam's face**. Billiam had heard him tell Evan he would *"get back to work."*
[[Lark]] caught him on the way down, pulled his hood up and kept hold of his arm.

When his magic surges, the aura is drawn back into his arm by **a bracelet he wears
under his sleeve**, which feeds it back into his casting. He told Aeska his
performance was great. At the yard he told the others he had not wanted to go into
the crowded pub in case something went wrong: when he casts, something takes hold of
him, *"it's like magic coming out of me but it's not mine,"* and he is worried things
will go bad again. *"Trust me, it can get really bad."* On Hollis and Alfie: *"I never
liked them anyway. Well, Alfie was all right, to be honest, but maybe he got dragged
in."* Lark told him he could not go alone any more, and asked to stay at his house;
he hesitated, then agreed.

At home he was not sure where a guest should sleep, so he made up a bed in his room
and another on the sofa, and waited up for her. She chose his room, so they could
talk. She asked whether the masked man scared him. He said it was frightening, what
the man had done, and that he hoped he was only a mercenary, since he had heard him
say he had to get back to work. Lark said Calder had gone pale and run, and that she
was worried. She told him she had never been part of a group like this, and that even
though it was dangerous she thought it was pretty cool. Billiam said he would not mind
things going back to normal: *"at least when I went to work, I kind of knew what I was
doing. And now I'm just always kind of lost."* Lark said he had great magic and they
should help him with it. He said he had tried, and that at the College the others
could learn spells from books and he never could. *"Billy, you need to have more
courage,"* she told him: *"You have to keep trying. And we're gonna be here for
you."* He said he would still only use it if he really had to, but he could try a bit
more. He asked her, if she met his mother, to say she worked at the yard too, because
his mother does not know it has stopped; and his father, he said, probably thinks he
is going to turn himself in.

At the yard the next morning he told Aeska he did not strike him as the trusting type
either. Aeska said he had improved from where he started, and that Billiam seemed
untrusting himself. Billiam felt they had had a good day. He was resentful that
Hollis and Alfie would steal from their boss, wanted to confront them, and thought
whatever they took might help Shrapnel.

At noon two men he did not know brought a crate to the gate that nobody
had ordered. When he asked for papers the older one pulled a cudgel and hit him
across the head. Billiam answered with a Thunderwave that threw both men, their cart
and the crate across the street. The driver lived. Out of the broken crate came
**an eight-foot creature of flesh and machine** with a greenish essence core in its
chest, and it rushed him.
