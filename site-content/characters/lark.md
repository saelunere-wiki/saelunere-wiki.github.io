# Lark

## At a glance

- **Race:** [[Aela]]
- **Pronouns:** she/her
- A courier for the Brassline crew.

## Appearance

Young and slight, androgynous, warm brown freckled skin, a wide easy smile. Short wavy two-toned hair - brown on one side, white-blonde on the other. Cream tunic, flowing sage-green scarf/cowl, brown trousers with leather/corded belts and a satchel, hands wrapped in bandages. Carries a tall cloth-wrapped staff/polearm.
