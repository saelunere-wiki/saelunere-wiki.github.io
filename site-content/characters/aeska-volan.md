# Aeska Volan

## At a glance

- **Race:** [[Aela]]
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- A wandering undercity musician.

## Appearance

Aela man, chestnut-brown hair swept up and back, light stubble with mustache and goatee, a sharp determined gaze, a radiant golden halo. Dressed down in the undercity: worn patched leather jacket, loose shirt, frayed scarf.

## Identity

**Aeska Volan.** Son of the Volan family; his father is second in command to
[[Dr. Mannsfield T. Tammerton|Dr. Tammerton]], and Aeska was raised to succeed Tammerton in
[[The Guild of Engineers]]. He has been **disowned by his family**.

He knew [[Theodore Blackwood]] during his guild years and counted him a friend, though they
had not spoken in two.

> *Stated by him to the other four at [[The Plumb and Bob]] in Episode 1. He did not say why
> he was disowned.*

## Connections

- Present in [[Oblong Square]] at Harvest Fest, Terr 00 756, when
  [[Theodore Blackwood]] was killed - see [[Episode 1 - Summary]].
