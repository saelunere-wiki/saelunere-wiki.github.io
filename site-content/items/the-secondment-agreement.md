# The Secondment Agreement

A contract between [[The College of Arcanographers]] and [[The Guild of Engineers]],
releasing [[Professor Benjamin Buckman]] to the Guild. [[Calder Flynn|Calder]] took it
from the Professor's office at the College on the night of Tide 05, 756.

## The text

> **THE COLLEGE OF ARCANOGRAPHERS**
> **and**
> **THE GUILD OF ENGINEERS**
>
> **Agreement of Secondment**
>
> Made this day, Terr 86, 756, between the College of Arcanographers and the Guild of Engineers, concerning Professor Benjamin Buckman of the College.
>
> 1. The Professor is released to the Guild for the whole of his time, from this day until the Work is complete or the Guild returns him.
> 2. The Professor will reside and work within the Guild's spire. He will be admitted by escort, and will receive no visitors there.
> 3. The College will continue the Professor's stipend. The Guild will pay the College the sum already agreed, each season, for the loss of him.
> 4. All notes, figures and instruments touching the Work are the property of the Guild and will not leave the spire. Any such papers held at the College have been delivered up under this agreement.
> 5. Letters addressed to the Professor will be received by the Guild's clerks and passed to him at their discretion.
> 6. The Professor's present student will continue on directed reading until his return.
> 7. The College will make no enquiry into the nature of the Work, now or after its completion.
>
> Signed for the College,
> *Edmund Whitaker*, Dean
>
> Signed,
> *B. Buckman*
>
> Signed for the Guild of Engineers,
> *V.V.*, Head of Operations

## What the party makes of it

Calder handed it to [[Aeska Volan|Aeska]] at [[The Cobbler's Pot]]: *"I think this is
your father's signature down there."* The five take *"V.V., Head of Operations"* for
[[Valerin Volan]]. To Aeska it was one more contract of the kind he used to handle,
and confirmed what they already believed. To [[Billiam Buckman|Billiam]] it showed
what his father is doing and why he is never at home.
