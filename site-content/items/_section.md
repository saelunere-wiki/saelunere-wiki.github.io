This section is for **campaign-significant items only** - named artifacts, plot
objects, essence relics, and anything the party will want to remember later.
Everyday loot, coin, and ordinary gear don't belong here.

To add one, create `items/<name>.md` with `type: item` in the frontmatter, then
link it from the sessions, characters, or factions it matters to - it'll show up
here automatically.
