# The Seeking Watch

A pocketwatch that has been altered: a fragment of an essence core is set into it,
and it gives off a faint green glow.

## What it does

A caster sets it by **naming a spell**. For up to an hour afterwards the needle shows
the direction of the **nearest casting of that spell**, at any range. Once it is set
it can be handed to someone else, and it goes on pointing. The working is a variant of
Locate Object called **Locate Magic**.

A common spell is no use to it. In a city where nearly everyone can manage a cantrip,
the nearest casting could be anybody's. It wants a spell that few people cast.

## How the party came by it

[[Felix Klaudius|Felix]] found it on the cobbles outside [[Claringbold's Yard]] on
Tide 05, 756, fallen from the pocket of the older of the two men who brought the
crate. On the man was a note, which he had been told to burn:

> *Take this and deliver the crate nearby. See that it is delivered. Do not be seen
> doing it, do not linger, and do not stay to watch.*
>
> *This piece comes back with you. It cannot be replaced in good order, and it is worth
> a great deal more than you are. If you lose it, do not come back at all.*
>
> *Burn this note.*

When Felix took the watch out, **the needle was pointing at him**, and it went on
pointing at him when Billiam held it. By late afternoon it was only turning gently.
[[Aeska Volan|Aeska]] identified it that evening, and can use it himself.

## What the party does not know

- **Which spell was named.** The watch was following a casting, not Felix as such.
  Aeska's question is which of Felix's spells is rare enough to lead it to him.
- **Who set it.** The man carrying it was a hired hand, told to bring it back.
