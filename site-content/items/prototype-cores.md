# Prototype Cores

Essence cores of a new kind, more powerful than an ordinary core. They come out of
prototype machines that should never have left [[The Guild of Engineers]]. One such
machine reached [[Claringbold's Yard]], where [[Billiam Buckman|Billiam]] and
[[Shrapnel]] stripped it; Shrapnel called its cores odd, and took the box away
himself.

[[The Blueprints]] from Professor Buckman's office show what they are made for: an
engine that sets a great many of them together.

## The ten the party holds

After Shrapnel was taken, [[Hollis]] and [[Alfie]] cut open his safe and split a box
of these cores between them. On Tide 06, 756, Billiam found Alfie's share in a linen
sack in his room: **ten prototype cores.** Sold as ordinary cores they would fetch
three hundred ducats easily, and they are worth more than that.

Hollis has not sold his share. By Alfie's account he is keeping it at
[[The Firebox]], and looking for a buyer.
