# The Blueprints

A set of technical drawings, written in Technic, for a piece of essence machinery.
[[Calder Flynn|Calder]] took them from [[Professor Benjamin Buckman]]'s office at
[[The College of Arcanographers|the College]] on the night of Tide 05, 756. They meant
nothing to him.

## What they show

[[Aeska Volan|Aeska]] reads Technic, and the five went through them together the next
day. The design is advanced and proprietary, a prototype, and parts of it made no
sense even to him. What could be read:

- **It is an engine**, or a generator. It sets a great many **prototype cores**
  together to make a huge amount of power in a small space, with fewer cores than that
  would normally take.
- **[[Billiam Buckman|Billiam]] knew the drawing.** It is the same kind of machine he
  and [[Shrapnel]] took apart at [[Claringbold's Yard]] before
  [[Theodore Blackwood|Theo]] was shot.
- **One serial number on it matches one in Theo's ledger.**

## What the party makes of it

Aeska's reading: Theo must have sent one of these machines to the yard on purpose, and
whatever Billiam's father was working on was important enough for Theo to send it
somewhere, and important enough to get him killed. What the engine is for, they do not
know.
