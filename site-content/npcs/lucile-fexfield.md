# Lucile Fexfield

Runs [[Fexfield Scrappers]] with her brother, [[Hugh Fexfield|Hugh]]. Their father ran it before them, and
they keep it in the family and among people they know - *"I'm obviously Fexfield,
it's on the door."* She works upstairs, in a room more workshop than office.

Blunt, chatty and dry: *"we get a lot of chancers come in here... it's not easy to
separate the wheat from the chaff, whatever wheat is, we don't have wheat in the
city."* She always needs people, but does not like being asked about guilds, and
does not trust strangers who might be guild spies or from a rival yard.

## Shrapnel

She is a friend of [[Shrapnel]]'s - *"a good lad, we do a bit of work together"* -
and once she knew Billiam and Felix were his, she closed the door herself and
talked:

- Shrapnel had **a couple of contracts that he kept off the books**, with mutual
  friends of hers, and they were lucrative.
- **The Porter's Guild are the buyers.** The arrangement is kept from
  [[The Corewright's Association]], who would take a big percentage if they knew.
- The two who took Shrapnel, a big woman and a blonde man, are the ones who collect
  the merchandise and make the payments. **They came to her yard that morning** and
  she sent them away.
- **The supply is limited.** *"They pay money, they don't need money, they need the
  cores."* If Shrapnel has not delivered his part they cannot get it anywhere else,
  so he **might be traded back** for the goods.
- If he is held anywhere, it is the Porters' spire - *"and good luck getting in
  there."*

*"You take care of Shrapnel, all right? He's a good guy."*
