# Barry Killerman

An older man in a flat cap who works one of the forges in [[The Core|the Core]]
and drinks at [[The Plumb and Bob]] often enough that the landlord knows his name
and his trade.

A regular at the back-room meetings [[Theodore Blackwood]] held there, and
unembarrassed about why: *"he was my hero, he was."* What he valued was that a man
that senior in the guild would stand and talk with the rest of them.

He missed the Harvestfest speech because he had been drinking in another pub down
the way, and is sheepish about it. He does not believe the rumours that Theo had
criminal connections.

## What he saw

**A couple of weeks before the killing, two strangers came into the Plumb and Bob,
went straight to Theo and argued with him loudly.**

- An older grey-haired woman in a big green overcoat.
- A younger Aela man with scars on his face.

Smartly dressed, not from around there, and not friendly. Not criminals to his
eye, but not people he wanted to catch the eye of either.

## Relationships

- **Attended** [[Theodore Blackwood]]'s meetings at [[The Plumb and Bob]]
- **Spoke to** [[Lark]] the evening the two strangers came back
