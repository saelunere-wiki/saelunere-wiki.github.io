# Penn

One of two people [[Calder Flynn|Calder]] meets in [[The Bellows]] by prior
arrangement, who call him **boss** and **"Mr. C"**. Small, young and ratty-looking,
beside [[Dunmore]]'s bulk.

In Episode 5 Calder gave Penn the easier job: **watch the Volan building in
[[The Outlines]]** and report who goes in and who comes out, and do not go inside.

- **Works for** [[Calder Flynn]]
- **Works with** [[Dunmore]]
