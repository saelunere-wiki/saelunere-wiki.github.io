# Denton

A courier at [[The Brassline]], and one of [[Lark]]'s old crewmates. A tall, lanky
[[Darkin]] with blue-grey skin and small curved horns. One of his arms is a
prosthetic, without much range of movement in it.

## Relationships

- **Works for** [[Mira]], at [[The Brassline]]
- **Worked alongside** [[Lark]]
- **Is seeing** [[Annie Buckman]]

## Episode 8

On the night of Tide 05 he opened the depot's side door to Lark. *"I thought you got
fired?"* *"I didn't get fired. I resigned."* He would not say at first whether Mira was
in, then gave way: *"all right, all right, I'm just pulling your leg."* He said he had
had a late run.

The next afternoon [[Billiam Buckman|Billiam]] kicked open his mother's bedroom door
and found Denton in her bed. Lark, looking in behind him, knew him at once. Billiam
struck at his mind with a spell and then went at him with his fists. **Denton covered
up and did not try to hurt him.** He hit back only when nothing was stopping it:
*"I'm not trying to fight anyone... Get this fucking kid off me, man."* Lark shoved
him, and then swept his legs from under him with her staff. The episode ends with him
on the floor, held in place by Aeska's magic.
