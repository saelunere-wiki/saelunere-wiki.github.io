# Harl Mott

The barman at [[The Plumb and Bob]] on [[Oblong Square]], and well known to
anyone who drinks there. A big man of sixty, perpetually harassed behind the bar,
missing his left leg below the knee and long past thinking about it.

He closed the tavern for [[Theodore Blackwood]]'s speech and pushed his customers
out into the square to listen, as did every other landlord on the square.

Afterwards he took the party in and hid them. **Aeska paid him 10 ducats to have
never seen them**, which he took cheerfully, being no friend of the guilds, and
he offered to tell anyone who came asking that it had been [[The Below Boys]].
