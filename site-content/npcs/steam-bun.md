# Steam Bun

One of the two Bullyboy [[Constructs|constructs]] that work at
[[Claringbold's Yard]], named by [[Billiam Buckman|Billiam]] after steamed buns.
**Very old**, well past the normal working life of a construct, and kept going for
years by Billiam's tinkering.

## After the raid

Found on its side on the morning of Wexdae, steam coming out of it and one leg
still turning as if trying to walk. **It was hit with something heavy** at the top
of the shoulder, and cannot get up. Billiam spent the morning on it and Aeska tried
after him; neither could get it back on its legs. A construct is not alive, so it
will not get any worse - it simply cannot work.
