# Annie Buckman

[[Billiam Buckman|Billiam]]'s mother, and a nurse at [[The Institute of Physikers]].
A human woman of thirty-seven, short and solidly built, with the steady,
unhurried movements of someone used to handling people who are frightened. A
round face, freckled across the nose, light brown hair pinned up off her collar
and always half-loose by the end of a shift. She wears the Institute's plain grey
nursing smock with the sleeves rolled to the elbow, sensible boots, and no
jewellery but a thin, worn wedding band.

**She was on call at the Institute the night of Harvestfest.** The staff there
expected [[Theodore Blackwood]] to be brought in for healing and waited for him,
and **he never arrived** - which none of them can explain.

Her husband, [[Professor Benjamin Buckman]], has not been home in days, sleeping
at his work on some kind of project, and she is quietly unhappy about it.
