# Professor Benjamin Buckman

A professor at [[The College of Arcanographers]], and by the account of those who
studied under him a capable magic user. **He takes one research student at a
time.**

- [[Calder Flynn]] is the current one, placed with him through
  [[Theodore Blackwood]].
- [[Billiam Buckman|Billiam]] was his apprentice before Calder, and is his son.

**He is currently off-site indefinitely, working on an unspecified project at
[[The Guild of Engineers]]**, and has been sleeping at his work rather than at
home for some days. He left word that Calder should carry on unsupervised until
the project is complete and he returns.

Neither his son nor his student was able to reach him in the two days after the
killing.

## Relationships

- **Supervises** [[Calder Flynn]]
- **Father of** [[Billiam Buckman]], and taught him before Calder
- **Currently working at** [[The Guild of Engineers]]

## He came home

For one night, on Tide 02, 756, for one errand - **research he keeps stored at
the house** - and then he left again.

**He knew [[Aeska Volan|Aeska]] on sight.** Before saying anything aloud he
offered a handshake, and used the extended hand to speak to him silently,
telepathy of some kind, asking what he was doing there. To his son it looked only
like two men holding eye contact a beat too long.

Out on the balcony, to [[Billiam Buckman|Billiam]]:

- Forget you saw anything. Do not get involved. Go back to your life.
- **Get rid of Aeska.** House Volan; the man who was to succeed
  [[Dr. Mannsfield T. Tammerton|Dr. Tammerton]] at [[The Guild of Engineers]];
  expelled, and **a wanted man** whose own family would like him removed from the
  equation.
- **Turn yourself in to the Guild of Engineers.** Tell the truth, take a couple of
  years, perhaps only months of hard labour. **Give them Aeska**, including where
  he is sleeping, and they may go easier on you.
- *"They do not care whether you did it or not, but they will remove you from the
  board one way or another."*

He was explicit that he **has no sway at the guild**. If it were
[[The College of Arcanographers|the College]] holding his son, Billiam would live
in comfort; at the guild he can promise nothing.

Asked why he still works with people he describes that way, he said the work is
important - *"this is revolutionary"* - that the guild are the only ones in a
position to make it happen, and that if he did not do it somebody else would,
with a worse moral compass.

He also said that **there are people inside the Guild of Engineers who can remove
a person's magic**, and that it is likely how they would deal with Billiam if he
became a problem. *He is the only source for that.*

Leaving the house again that night, he told Billiam to think about his choices over
the coming days. He said he is **very close to having everything finished at the
Guild**, and will be back and more available after that. Until then Billiam can
reach him by a sending or a letter, and should stay away from the guild as much as
possible.

## His letter to Calder

His reply to Calder's letter arrived at the College the next afternoon. Most of it
was research. To Calder's question about powerful essence weapons he wrote:
*"Calder, I recommend that you stop this line of inquiry. It's not relevant to your
college duties."* The practical application of magic for aggressive purposes such
as weapons, he added, is not something the College likes to delve into.

## Valerin Volan

[[Aeska Volan|Aeska]] says Buckman **worked closely with his father,
[[Valerin Volan]]**, on projects neither of them ever told Aeska about. Billiam has
been through his father's things and found something marked with a V, and it was
recent.
