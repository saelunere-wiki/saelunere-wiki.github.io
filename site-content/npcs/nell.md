# Nell

Works at [[Salador's Slip]] and lives in one of its rooms, second on the left. A
young [[Darkin]] woman of about [[Lark]]'s own age, with short black hair, horns
curling back from her head, and a big knitted cardigan. She and Lark are friends,
and Nell is the person Lark talks to most at the house.

The workers sleep late, because the house does not open until two or three in the
afternoon.

## The night Lark turned up

Woken by Lark's knock at two in the morning, she pulled her into her tiny one-bed
room and would not let her sleep on the floor: they shared.

The next morning Lark told her that [[Madame Rooke]] had threatened consequences
for Nell too, if Lark let her down. *"I don't want that to happen either,"* Nell
said. Maybe Rooke was just talking - *"let's not find out."* She thinks Rooke
would not give Lark a job like that unless she liked her, and was probably testing
her.

Lark gave her a short version of her troubles, with no names in it. *"That's so
exciting. I never get to do anything exciting."*

## Relationships

- **Friend of** [[Lark]]
- **Works for** [[Madame Rooke]]
- **Listening for** four names, for [[Lark]]

## Episode 7

Lark found her in her room in the afternoon. She was glad about the job, and said
rooms at the house are tight. Lark asked her to listen at the bathhouse for four
names: Evan Langford, Sasha Langford, Gertrude and Alistar. Nell said that listening
is what they do already: the workers tell [[Madame Rooke]] what they hear, and Rooke
knows it. They settled that Nell will tell Rooke as usual and tell Lark too, without
telling Rooke that Lark asked. *"I can be nonchalant."* She asked whether Lark was
going to beat someone up.
