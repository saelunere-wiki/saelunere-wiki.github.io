# Orson Thorne

A human man of eighty, quite tall but grown fragile with age. Wild, unkempt
white hair, and fingers permanently stained with old ink from a lifetime spent
writing out magical theory. His eyes are still sharp and piercing, though they
occasionally go glassy and distant when his mind slips. He wears an old,
threadbare Arcanographer’s robe, its cuffs a little singed and its pockets
stretched out of shape from sixty years of carrying heavy books.

A very old, wizened man, and an old friend of the Flynn family. He lives in a
small side house on the Flynns' plot - one room stacked floor to ceiling with
books, a small bed, and scrolls open on every surface - and [[Johan Flynn|Johan]]
and [[Vanessa Flynn|Vanessa]] look in on him every day.

A retired magic scholar. **On magical theory he is entirely present and
formidable**, and will follow the most complicated problem anyone can put to him,
drawing diagrams as he goes. On anything else his memory is visibly going, and he
will ask the same question twice in a single visit.

## On the weapon

Given the problem of throwing an essence blast a long distance and still landing
it accurately, he worked it through with [[Calder Flynn]] and concluded that it
would take enough energy to need **a great many essence cores and a great deal of
money**.

> Wouldn't just happen in the basement of some old house somewhere.
