# Mira

A large, buxom, blonde woman of forty. Runs [[The Brassline]], the courier crew [[Lark]] ran deliveries for. She no
longer goes out on deliveries herself and spends most of the day in her office
above the depot floor.

Told that [[Theodore Blackwood]] had been killed, that Lark had been there, and
that she meant to find out what happened, Mira's advice was to keep away from
the guild lot if she possibly could - and that she already sounded more involved
than she should be.

She would not hold the bunk or the job open. She is not anyone's mother, she has
a business to run, and she cannot be a hand short for nine or ten days because a
courier has decided she is a detective. **She took the returned Brassline pin and
put it in her desk drawer**, and looked sad doing it. If Lark does not get herself
killed with whatever she is following, she said, she should come back.
