# Tobin Marsh

A junior engineer at [[The Guild of Engineers]], and once [[Aeska Volan|Aeska]]'s
aide there.

Of everyone at the guild he is remembered as **the one who stayed loyal after
Aeska was expelled** - though there has been no contact in two years, and no way
of knowing what became of him or what he does there now.

He lived in a guild tenement in [[The Core|the Core]], most likely one of those
near the guild's spire.

Aeska has quietly put word out through [[Harl Mott]] to find him.

## Relationships

- **Was** [[Aeska Volan]]'s aide at [[The Guild of Engineers]]
- **Being sought by** Aeska, through [[Harl Mott]]
