# Emory

The doorman at [[Salador's Slip]]. Young, thin, with sandy hair that will not lie
flat, round spectacles and a dark coat. **He does not look like a bouncer**, and
nobody would bet on him winning a fight - though the Slip does not have the kind of
reputation where fights happen often.

## The night Lark turned up

He was nodding off at the door at half past midnight, there to see late guests
out rather than let anyone in. He turned [[Lark]] away, bloody from the Bellows:
*"we're trying to run a high class establishment here."* When she tried to dash
past him he caught her round the waist, and **he is surprisingly strong for how
he looks.** Then he went and fetched [[Delia]] rather than deal with it himself.
