# Shepherd

One of the two Bullyboy [[Constructs|constructs]] that work at
[[Claringbold's Yard]], named by [[Billiam Buckman|Billiam]] after shepherd's pie.
A headless, horse-like hauling frame with flat shoulders and a big lamp between
them. It comes forward every morning to nuzzle into Billiam with its shoulders.

## After the raid

On the morning of Wexdae it came limping out to meet Billiam, **one leg gone**,
and nudged him round the corner to where [[Steam Bun]] lay. It still walks, badly.
Neither Billiam nor Aeska could repair it.

[[Felix Klaudius|Felix]] spoke with it. What it said:

- *"Big bad time. Bang, bang, bang."*
- The attackers: *"big, green coat, blonde man, very fast."*
- [[Shrapnel]]: *"Boss taken, yes."*
- Whether they carried anything out of the office: *"No. Smash, smash."*

## Relationships

- **Works at** [[Claringbold's Yard]], alongside [[Steam Bun]]

## Episode 6

Asked by Felix who had been in the yard after Billiam left: *"Hollis. Alfie."* Were there
noises? *"Noises. Yes, yes."*

## Episode 7

When the creature came out of the crate, Shepherd fled from it. Then it went to
Billiam where he lay at the gate, nudged him with its lamp, hooked its lamp head round
him and dragged him back towards the office.
