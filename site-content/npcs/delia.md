# Delia

The senior of the workers at [[Salador's Slip]], something like an older sister to
the rest of them. Dark hair worn loose and usually damp from the steam, and a
direct, unimpressed look.

## The night Lark turned up

Still working and cleaning up at half past midnight when [[Emory]] fetched her to
the door. She pushed him out of the way, told [[Lark]] she looked a mess, and let
her in - on the understanding that it is [[Madame Rooke]] Lark has to make it up
to, not Delia. She took Lark's offer to clean the house in place of paying, and
said she would tell Madame Rooke that Lark turned up and refused to leave until
she had helped.

*"Just don't fuck anything up. Because that'll be on my head."*
