# Evan Langford

The son of [[Sasha Langford]], head of [[The Porter's Guild]]. A nobleman to look at:
a green suit in the guild's colour, jewellery, slicked-back hair, and **glowing
yellow eyes**.

[[Aeska Volan|Aeska]] placed him from the name, his age, and a vague memory of
having seen him at a party or two in his own noble years.

## The room at the end of the corridor

On Lumesdae night, Tide 04, 756, Aeska found him in the best private room above
[[The Head of Steam]], behind the frosted glass, playing cards with three others:
[[The woman in green|the woman in green]], [[The scarred man|the blonde Aela man]]
who travels with her, and a man cloaked in black in a porcelain white mask. Evan was
**winning everything**, a mountain of ducats in front of him and the rest of the
table cleaned out, and was openly bored by his company: *"These lot here are
useless."*

He loves music. When Aeska, disguised and calling himself Valor, was sent up to
play, Evan was delighted - *"why are we skimping on the luxuries of life? Music is
an art"* - applauded, and waited for the others to clap before they did. He asked
whether Valor plays dice, gave his own name, and invited him back any time he is in
the area.
