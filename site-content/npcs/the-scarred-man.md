# The scarred man

A young Aela man, lean and hard-faced, with a scar running down through both lips
and the chin. He wears the same heavy forest green and the same brass
[[The Porter's Guild|Porter's Guild]] pin as
[[The woman in green|the woman he arrived with]].

**He did not speak once.** He stepped forward a single time, when she was not
getting the answer she wanted, and otherwise stood at the tavern door watching the
room to see whether anybody would follow her out.

He is almost certainly the younger scarred Aela man [[Barry Killerman]] saw
arguing with [[Theodore Blackwood]] in the same tavern a couple of weeks before he
died.

## Relationships

- **Works with** [[The woman in green]]
- **Watched the room** at [[The Plumb and Bob]]

## In the square

He ran Lark down from behind and struck her once in the back of the head, hard
enough that she could not move or speak. Then he pulled **a length of rope off his
own belt**, looped it over her head, and set about hauling her backwards with it,
apparently indifferent to whether she survived being dragged.

Half blacked out, with the rope at her throat, **she asked him whether he was
going to kill her just for a name.**

**He looked her in the eyes and said nothing.**

He has now been in two scenes with the party and has not spoken once. There is no
anger in how he fights and no enjoyment of it. Felix eventually froze him
mid-stride as he started after Lark, and the last thing he did before the spell
took him was turn his eyes onto Felix. He was still frozen when
[[Billiam Buckman|Billiam]] sprinted past and spat in his face.

## At Evan's table

The morning after the raid on [[Claringbold's Yard]], the two of them called at
[[Fexfield Scrappers]] asking questions and were sent away. That night he
was in the best private room above [[The Head of Steam]], playing cards with
[[Evan Langford]] and a man in a porcelain mask, and losing.

When Evan cued the table to applaud Aeska's song, he gave a slow clap with no
expression on his face at all.
