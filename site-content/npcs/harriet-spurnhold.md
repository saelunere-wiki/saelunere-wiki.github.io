# Harriet Spurnhold

Previously Chief Secretary at [[The Guild of Engineers]], senior over the guild's
secretarial and administrative staff. Small, upright and precise, with a narrow
pinched face, a sharp nose, and deep vertical lines between the brows from a
lifetime of disapproving. Greying hair scraped back into a hard low bun with a
pen pushed through it, and small round spectacles she looks over rather than
through. Ink on her cuffs, a chatelaine of small keys at her belt, and a ledger
held against her chest.

The morning after [[Theodore Blackwood]] was killed, **she locked his office and
stood in the corridor trying to hold off the clerks and guardsmen who had come to
search it**, insisting there were correct procedures to follow for a man who had
unfortunately passed away.

Later the same day she asked [[Felix Klaudius]] to sit in as interim chief of
Essence Transfer for a few weeks, until things were sorted.

**By the following morning she was no longer employed by the Guild of Engineers**,
and nobody at the spire would say why.
