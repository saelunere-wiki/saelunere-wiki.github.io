# Alfie

A young [[Darkin]] of about nineteen who worked at [[Claringbold's Yard]].
Excitable, and friendly with Billiam; the two of them are quite alike.

When the agents came into the yard - *"threatening and everything and super
scary"* - he **hid straight away in one of the buckets in the corner**, and when he
looked up again Shrapnel was gone and the office was smashed. His description: a big
woman in a big green coat, and a scary man with scars across his face and blonde
hair. *"Obviously guild guys."*

He came back the next morning for his wages. *"I gotta work, Billy. I can't wait
around for Shrapnel. We're not gonna get paid."*
