# The Baron

Landlord of [[The Head of Steam]]. Tall, balding and large, with a monocle in his
right eye that he uses to look down his nose at people like a nobleman - before
breaking into a big grin. His left arm is gone, and the sleeve is stitched up.

*"They call me the Baron."* His actual name, he admits if asked, is Barry.

He was a porter "since almost the city began". Hauling a crate up the side of a
warehouse in [[The Core|the Core]], it fell and took the arm off - "quick and
easy". He retired on a payout from the guild and set up the pub, and the Porters,
who respect one of their own, have made it theirs. The spire is just over the way.

He once had a few musicians in and thought them bad and the noise no help, so there
is no live music. He is proud of his VIP guests, "some of the highest members of the
Porter's Guild", and easily flattered: on one good word he sent a stranger calling
himself Valor up to play for the room at the end, as a baptism by fire.
