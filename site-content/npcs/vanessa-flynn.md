# Vanessa Flynn

A human woman of fifty, strong-built, with a far more animated, expressive face
than [[Johan Flynn|Johan]] - bright, warm eyes always darting about, reading how
things work. Hair tied up in a messy, practical bun, often held with a forged
metal wrench or a brass hairpin of her own making. She wears a battered, heavy
engineer’s coat, its pockets forever bulging with odd mechanical parts,
scribbled notes and half-finished ideas.

[[Calder Flynn]]'s mother, an engineer on the forges alongside
[[Johan Flynn|Johan]]. Their house is half workshop and she likes it that way.

She put food on for Calder the moment he arrived although they had already eaten,
on the grounds that he is a growing boy. She and Johan look in on
[[Orson Thorne]] every day.

She had heard the rumours about [[Theodore Blackwood]]'s supposed criminal
dealings and was genuinely surprised by them, never having taken him for anything
of the kind. **She was visibly distressed to learn her son had been standing in
the square when it happened.**
