# Dunmore

One of two people [[Calder Flynn|Calder]] meets in [[The Bellows]] by prior
arrangement, who call him **boss** and **"Mr. C"**. Very big and muscular. Word
comes back from him by an agreed drop.

In Episode 5 Calder asked him to work the underground on [[The Porter's Guild]]:
whether they really have taken someone into custody, what has happened to him, and
where he is held - without going inside the guild.

**He pushed back.** A lot of the Porter's Guild, he said, is mixed up with the
underworld, unlike the Guild of Engineers, who are "all prim and proper" and do not
deal with people who work slightly around the law. Plenty of people in those
circles have their own connections to the Porters, so asking around too much will
cause a stink. Instead he pointed Calder at [[The Head of Steam]], the Porters'
clubhouse, which he and [[Penn]] cannot use themselves because they are known faces.
