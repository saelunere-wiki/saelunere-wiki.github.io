# Mister K

A man cloaked in black velvet, with a porcelain white mask, who never shows his hands.

## At Evan's table

In Episode 5 he was the fourth at [[Evan Langford]]'s card table in the best room above
[[The Head of Steam]], with [[Gertrude]] and [[Alistar]]. He watched [[Aeska Volan|Aeska]]
play without interest and did not clap.

## Through the wall

Leaving, he said *"Thank you for your time, Evan. I'll get back to work,"* and walked
out through the wall, two storeys up, standing on the air at the same height. He came
face to face with [[Billiam Buckman|Billiam]] on the ledge, as startled as Billiam was.
Billiam's spell did nothing to him; he waved it off, turned invisible and was gone,
fast, without striking back. **He has seen Billiam's face.** To [[Lark]], below in the
alley, he was a white face, a black cloak, and then nothing.

## What Calder told the party

[[Calder Flynn|Calder]] knows him. He told the others to stay very far away from him,
not to say his name, *"but he's called Mr. K."* Calder once got information for him for
money: a job at the Volan house, a purple pouch, the night of the fire. Mister K already
knew Calder's parents, by name and by their daily routine.
