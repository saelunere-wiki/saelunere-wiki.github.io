# Hollis

A human of about fifty who worked at [[Claringbold's Yard]] for a very long time,
knew [[Shrapnel]] well, and was the nearest thing the five-man crew had to a second
in command.

When the agents turned up, **Shrapnel dismissed the crew and told them to get out**,
and Hollis went home on his order. He is ashamed of it. His verdict to Billiam:
*"I wouldn't expect to see him again."*

He came back the next morning only for his wages. *"No offence to you, Billy, I
respect your dedication, but I'm looking for another job."* He went into the office
with Alfie to take what they were owed, and left.

## Relationships

- **Worked at** [[Claringbold's Yard]], under [[Shrapnel]]
- **Worked alongside** [[Billiam Buckman]] and [[Alfie]]

## Episode 6

That night the party found a safe behind a painting in the back office of
[[Claringbold's Yard]], cut open with a heavy welding torch over a couple of hours, and
empty. Through [[Felix Klaudius|Felix]], [[Shepherd]] named Hollis and Alfie as the ones
who had been there after Billiam left, and said there had been noises. The yard's
employee records give the party both men's addresses.

## Episode 7

The address he gave in the yard's records is [[The Firebox]], a boxing gym in the
Stacks. Billiam wants to start with him.

## Episode 8

By [[Alfie]]'s account, emptying the safe was Hollis's idea: they were owed wages, and
Shrapnel was not coming back. Hollis knew where the safe was. They split a box of
cores from it. **Hollis has not sold his share. He is keeping it at [[The Firebox]]**,
and looking for a buyer for both of them.

The party did not go to him. Aeska expects Alfie to warn him. Lark and Calder wanted
to get there first, and Billiam said there was no reason to: what they wanted was
information, and they had it.
