# The woman in green

A large, broad woman past middle age with greying hair, in a heavy forest green
overcoat. She wears **an official [[The Porter's Guild|Porter's Guild]] pin - the
guild's own, with its logo, and not a crew pin.** Under the coat, concealed, she
carries an essence weapon in a hip holster.

Two days after [[Theodore Blackwood]] was killed she walked into
[[The Plumb and Bob]] and addressed the whole room, asking who had known him.

> We just got some unresolved business with Theo, and on account of his death
> we're looking for someone who's able to finish that business for us.

She was **not interested in his meetings.** Told that [[Lark]] had only attended
them, her answer was that she did not care about the meetings - she wanted
somebody who knew Theo and **did business** with him.

When she did not get a name she took Lark out into [[Oblong Square]] and **put a
hand round her throat against a wall, in daylight, in front of passers-by.** Lark
broke free, kneed her, and ran.

She arrived with, and left with, [[The scarred man|a scarred Aela man]]. She is
almost certainly one of the two strangers [[Barry Killerman]] saw arguing with
Theo in the same tavern a couple of weeks before he died.
