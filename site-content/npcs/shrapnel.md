# Shrapnel

[[Billiam Buckman|Billiam]]'s boss, and the man who runs [[Claringbold's Yard]],
the breakers' and construct-maintenance yard on the edge of [[The Core|the Core]].

A broad man of about fifty gone heavy at the middle, with forearms still ropey
from a lifetime of lifting, close-cropped iron-grey hair and a blunt jaw under
several days of stubble. Old pitted scars run up the left side of his neck and
jaw, and the top joints of two fingers are missing from his left hand. He dresses
for the floor: a scorched leather apron, a rag permanently over one shoulder,
smoked goggles pushed up on his forehead. Almost everyone calls him Shrapnel.

**Twenty-odd years of watching the guilds behave badly have left him difficult to
shock.** Told that [[Theodore Blackwood]] had been shot and that
[[The Guild of Engineers]] had carried the body off, his answer was that the
guilds mess with everyone's business and always have, and that somebody had to
take it - who else was going to, leave it to be looted?

He allowed that a man shot mid-speech at Harvestfest was wrong, and was not
otherwise troubled by it.
