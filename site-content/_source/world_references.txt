
**Geography and Cosmology**
```
Saelunere literally is an orb floating in a space-like void. The firmament is a solid spherical barrier of force that keeps the water, landmass and atmosphere of the world within it. This is understood to be fact by almost everyone, and so whilst in the past an explorer or two has set out into the surrounding sea to hit the firmament, most readily understand this is simply reality and there is nothing to be gained by attempting to explore further. 

There is no sun or moon in the sky, with light simply growing over time of day and diminishing, and night time is always very dark with no moon or other light sources. Stars are visible in the sky far off in the void, though nothing is known of their purpose or origin.
```

📅 Calendar Overview
• Current Year: 756
• The Corruption Began: 728 (after the death of Aelia)

• Year Length: 400 days
• Each Season: 100 days

──────────────────────────────────────

🌦️ Seasons
• Aela  (Spring) - Named after the mother goddess
• Ember (Summer) - Named for the fires of Yuna
• Terr  (Autumn) - Named for Erethan’s earth
• Tide  (Winter) - Named for the storms of Salador

──────────────────────────────────────

🗓️ Date Format
• Written as: [Season] [Day]
• Example: Aela 22 → 22nd day of Aela
