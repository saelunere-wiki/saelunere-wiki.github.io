**The origins of Saelunere (as told by The Faith of the Four Kin)**


```
In the beginning there was a black orb, floating in an endless white void. Four siblings found the orb.

Aelia pierced the orb with a spear of pure light, golden in radiance, and the hole she made flooded the orb with a beautiful dawn. But the light shone on nothingness, and had no purpose.

So Erethan fired his bow into the orb, the shaft a curling vine, the arrow tip a blossoming bud, and as the arrow struck the ground inside great fonts of life sprung forth across the surface. But the life was parched and dry, and could not flourish.

So Salador struck the orb with a hammer of pure water, each impact sending crashing waves forth, bringing water to nourish the life within. But the waves froze to ice, the rays of light not enough to fight the crippling cold of the void.

So Yuna slashed a sword of flame through the orb, and many fires burst into existence where her blade touched, the warmth of the fires thawed the ice and brought balance, allowing cycles to begin 

The siblings were happy with their work and entered the orb, where they created the creatures of the world and guided them to flourish.

But Valar, their cousin, had grown jealous of their creation. He stole his way across the void and placed his hand over the hole that Aelia had pierced, creating night, darkness and poisoning the minds within with evil. 

The four siblings, now indebted to the world they had created, left to challenge Valar, fighting back against his influence as best they can. Aelia fights daily to bring the light of day, Erethan creates life to outpace the endless march of death, Salador pushes back the tides that he once created and Yuna warms the ice cold frost to bring the harvest seasons.
```


**Theocide**

```
28 years ago, Aelia died. 

Every person watched as the warrior of light was cut down, a black gash opening her from shoulder to hip. It took many months, a slow and agonizing death witnessed by those below. At first they hoped it was just a flesh wound, until the cut became a ravine, dark ichor pouring out for many weeks until it covered the sky in days of eclipse. 

A few weeks later, the other Gods discovered their fallen sister and the cry they made broke everyone who heard it, a deep and mournful Godswail plucked from titanic throats that lasted many days and turned some to madness from empathy alone.

After that, nothing was the same. Light no longer had it’s same radiant quality, shadows dwelt uncomfortably, plants withered and the land greyed. All the while Aelia’s corpse lay floating out in the void, slowly getting smaller and smaller as she drifted away. Worst still, within a year, the liquid that was once her lifeblood, collided with the outside of Saelunere.

The dark black splatter hit the outside of the orb and began to leak inside. Rain turned pure black, acrid and withering. Whole cities of the continent died overnight, the air heavy with choking black dust and their corpses riddled with grey veins and mutations.

People survived, as they always do. Some through protective magics, others great feats of engineering, others still purely on luck and determination. Over time, the survivors gathered to the safest places, and built and expanded on what was left.

The first child born after the cry of the gods, was an Aasimar. From that point onwards, all children born were either Aasimar or Tieflings, seemingly at random - known as Aela and Darkin.

**Aela**

```
Aasimar (known as Aela) are one of the two races born after the theocide of Aelia. Named due to their innate angelic qualities and light-bearing skills that remind people of the mother goddess herself. Aela come in three forms, the ability to sprout heavenly wings, to burn brightly with radiant light and to be shrouded in a dark shroud. Aela resemble humans but often exhibit ‘angelic’ qualities such as glittering freckles, luminous eyes or radiant skin tones. 
```

**Darkin**
```
Darkin also come in three forms, although not directly seen to be related to a particular source, given the random nature of their birth, there are some older folk who distrust darkin and relate their more devilish features to the evil aspects of Valar. 

Despite this most simply see it as a quirk of birth, much like aela. Darkin typically also exhibit three forms, with some having a resistance to poison and sickness, others greater control and resistance to fire than normal, and others seemingly resistant to death itself. Darkin have a resemblance to ‘devils’, with some growing horns, tails, tusks and glowing eyes.

**The Seven Cities**
```
The majority of the landmass of Saelunere is corrupted by the spilt blood of Aelia. A dark and black mucus spreads throughout the ground, killing or severely wilting plants, poisoning water and killing most wildlife. Animals and humans unlucky enough to survive, through slow exposure or happenstance, have mutated into corrupted zombie-like beasts.

**Parvo**
```
Parvo, a city of spires, surrounds the chimney of a power plant, powered by a huge essence engine. The poorer districts have built up over time with their buildings having their backs to the chimney, its warmth a free comfort through ever colder nights. Richer residents build towers further from the chimney, the outer ring of the city being the most sought after to get as far away from the humidity and pollution of the center. 

The city is roughly circular, a consequence of a once planned out construction. It features a number of districts, known and named over time as the population swelled with refugees fleeing the outside. 

Parvo was originally the base site of a huge essence engine and surrounding mechanisms, designed to create and supply heat to the many other settlements built up around the cooler highlands where it is situated. This heat was pumped out not only for the benefits of heating homes, but to power mining and other industrial processes further up the mountain side.

The large curtain wall surrounding the complex was originally designed to seal any wayward essence eruptions or other mishaps contained from the outside world. However, after theocide and the rain of blood, these walls served to guard the interior against the brunt of corrupted creatures and it’s elevated position protected it from runoff. Although not completely covered from rain, the engineers within the complex were quick to patch roofs and add additional shelter over the industrial buildings, creating a contained space where very little of the corruption could seep in through rainfall.

Parvo became a safe haven, and the surrounding settlements quickly flooded in, the engineering guilds that had worked in the facility unwilling or simply unable to turn them away.
```

**The Bellows**
```
The lowest level of the city, below ground level, is a warren of pipework, steam vents and essence engines, originally designed to allow the industrial areas topside easy access to the facilities of the city. 

Since space is now a premium, many nooks, crannies and tunnels have become residential areas. The difficult navigation and maze-like layout naturally favouring criminals and those looking not to be found. 

However, there are still many engineers and maintenance workers in the Bellows that live below simply to be close to their work.

More recently a gang of upstarts calling themselves the Below Boys have started charging tolls to those travelling through the underground spaces.
```

**The Stacks**
```
The stacks refers to the collection of residential districts that have been built up the side of the main chimney stack in the center of the city. The concrete of the chimney remains warm during all hours, giving residences a free form of heating though proximity to the stack. The houses, shops and community buildings are built on top of each other, with spare space between them used as balconies, gardens and thoroughfares. 

Although one might expect a particularly messy arrangement, the engineering prowess of the city has lead to a relatively organized, well constructed section of buildings, with walkways, ladders and spiraling ramps connecting different tiers and areas. Pulley systems and chutes allow goods for the shops to travel up and down, and life continues vertically just as it does in the regular groundside districts below.

Whilst travelling all the way up to the highest buildings can be a tiresome feat, some residents have everything they need in the stacks, and rarely travel down to the ground, creating insular communities high above the hustle and bustle below.

This close to the constantly operating essence engine, the air is humid and thick, with a misty runoff that is not quite pollutant, but is not quite pure air either. For this reason, and the awkwardness of navigating vertically in this district, the houses here are far less in demand and tend to be inhabited by the poorer parts of the city and the waves of new refugee communities that entered after the theocide.
```

**The Furlong**
```
The Furlong is a ring road that travels around the outside of the chimney stack and the associated plant buildings, the main route through the city and where the majority of traffic is forced to pass through to get to various inner or outer areas of the city's circular shape. 

Government buildings, city infrastructure and large warehouses tend to lie directly roadside, places that require constant easy access or process the greatest volume of traffic.

There are many pedestrian bridges and tunnels over and under the furlong, meaning that for many residents and businesses it is simply quicker to have porters or constructs haul goods on foot between locations, to avoid joining the heavily trafficked thoroughfare.

Many joke that the furlong is technically an infinite traffic jam, two lines of slow moving carts and trucks that connect end to end, forever circling the city. Although, in the very early hours of the morning the volume of vehicles dies down enough that there are visual gaps in traffic and even meters of clear sections in some rare cases.

Around the circumference of the Furlong, built on poles above it, is the ‘Circular’ - An essence-powered tram originally designed for coal carts, that has since been modified to allow passengers as well as cargo to be loaded on it, to avoid the traffic below. It is a ring of carriages constantly moving, requiring people to hop on and off, but meaning that large bulky goods or delicate cargo isnt suitable for this sort of transportation.
```

**The Spires**
```
Dotted around the outside of the city, as close to the outside as can be managed, the rich and powerful build tall towers. Here, the air is as clear and cool as it can be whilst still protected by the city walls. These spires act not only as opulent residences, but as fully fledged estates and places of business for the guild leaders, merchant classes and political powers of Parvo.

The spires are usually laid out with residences for the family at the top, guest rooms, then living quarters and amenities, with servants quarters, infrastructure and business offices lower still. Many spires utilize complicated mechanical or essence-powered elevators and other contraptions to ensure ease of access to the many floors.

Due to a premium of space on the ground level of the city proper, most of the spires are very thin, only taking the footprint of a large family house at most, using masterful building techniques to create safe and sturdy structures that span seemingly impossible heights.

 The spires are architecturally beautiful, themed to the liking of the power owners, and designed as a statement to their peers. As such, some spires feature highly decorative facades, complicated design features and bright paintwork to stand out from the rest.
```





**The Seven Guilds**
```
Issues, laws and policy is designed by the leaders from various powerful guilds - known as the City Council - officially there are seven representatives from the major guilds, although often meetings will be attended by minor guild leaders and other representatives when expertise or informed opinion is required. Parvosians pride themselves on a meritocracy, the idea that those most suited to leading the city rise through the Guilds to have powerful voices on the council. In theory, when issues arrive that pertain to the expertise of a particular guild, their arguments should hold a greater weight and the debate should usually go in their favour.

**The Guilds**
```
The seven major guilds are the most prominent, and their leader or representative holds a seat on the City Council. Their remit is often larger than their name might suggest, although there are often smaller off-shoot guilds and factions within that deal with different specializations
```

**The Guild of Engineers**
_Head Engineer - Dr. Mannsfield T. Tammerton_
```
The Guild of Engineers is one of the biggest in the city, founded originally from the engineering and maintenance department of the Parvo Essence Plant. They encompass everything related to Essence Machinery in the city, from maintenance, renewal and innovation
```

**Corewright's Association**
_Corewright Kerwin Aberford_
```
A sub-department of the Guild of Engineers, the Corewright's Association deals with the creation and maintenance of Essence Cores, the devices that power and run constructs. A powerful faction, given the essential nature of constructs in the city and their more 'mysterious' functionality. 
```

**The Institute of Physikers**
_Surgeon General Dr. Maria Blackwell_
```
The Institute is the source of training and research in all things medical. Physikers serve an important and respected role as doctor's providing care to the sick and injured, as well as being at the forefront of the research into the effects of magical corruption.
```

**The Divine Calling**
_Father Manston Ulysses_
```
The divine arm of the Physikers, as devout kinsworn it's members focus more heavily on understanding the Gods, as well as espousing peace, tranquility and reflection in the name of the Four Kin.

**The Artificer's Union*
_Torman & Sons
Harriford Torman
The Longfellow Company
Julie Longfellow_

The Artificer's Union represents the many businesses and tradespeople that create, deal in or repair essence objects. As such there is often a change in spokesperson for the union, as different companies grow and wane in power. Currently Torman & Sons and the Longfellow Company have formed an uneasy coalition for representing the artificers of the city.
```

**The College of Arcanographers**
_Dean Edmund Whitaker (Professor)_
```
As close as the city has to a place of higher learning, Arcanographers deal with the science of magic and essence, researching it's effects and it's use outside of a pure power source. Generally lacking in respect from other guilds due to the more theoretical nature of their studies, they are generally less involved in politics and buried in their works instead.

```

**The Craftwork Consortium**
_Toolmaker's Guild
Tora Hargrave
The Order of Smiths
Maximilian Dulwich_
```
Despite being an essential part of city life, the various industries, businesses and workshops that make up the consortium are often looked down on due to the 'mundane' nature of their craft. None the less, each industry within tends to have a lucrative niche, a lot of employees and make good income, giving them a decent sway in city politics.
```

**The Porter's Guild**
_Headman Sasha Langford_
```
The Porter's Guild represents traders and couriers of all types within Parvo, especially when it comes to hauling goods on foot, as this still remains one of the most efficient ways to move cargo in the dense city. The other guilds strive to be on the Porter's good side, as that can lead to quicker routes and shorter queues.
```


**The Wainwright's Guild**
_Orson Knight_
```
An up and coming faction of the Porter's Guild, the Wainwright's deal with wheels and carts, but having recently overseen the implementation of the 'Circular' they now have a decent amount of control and oversight when it comes to traversing Parvo.
```


**The Water Company**
_Director Samuel Greaves_
```
Undoubtedly one of the most important guild's in the city, the Water Company strives to remain neutral to the factionalism of the City Council, in order to ensure that the population and industry that needs steady supply can recieve it. None the less, it's a thankless job given the scarcity of large volumes of clean water in the current age.

**Constructs**
```
Constructs are commonplace around the city - despite the availability of human labour, constructs have greater strength, better mobility and can be outfitted for more specialized tasks.  Most businesses and crews have one or two constructs that they use, whilst the bigger outfits can operate and maintain hundreds at a time.

Constructs are not sentient, and have a varying level of intelligence ranging from something similar to livestock up to a well trained workhorse or hound. In most cases, they have replaced beasts of burden and other animals that would take up too much space or resources to keep in Parvo.
```


**Types of Construct**
```
Although there are many variations and specializations, there are four very common types of construct that are seen in day to day life
```


**Bullyboy**
```
Bullyboys are quadrupedal beasts of burden, most often likened to a horse or ox.  They have large bodies and four legs, with no head. Instead between the shoulders of their front legs they have a large circular disc that functions as a lamp and all the necessary sensory organs. 

They are the least intelligent constructs, usually seen just standing completely idle until given very simple commands. They are used for haulage and as traction engines for tasks that require mobile engines. 
```


**Gladhand**
```
Gladhands resemble something between spiders and crabs, roughly the size of a wash basket, with ten legs arranged symmetrically around their circular torso. They have two stalk like antenna 'eyes' that give them a slightly goofy look, although many are creeped out by their arachnid-like features. 

With incredible dexterity, Gladhands are perfect for climbing around buildings and navigating gantries and ceilings where humans struggle. They are not very intelligent but can manage 'tighten that screw' and similar commands. Their spindly limbs are quite fragile, meaning they are relatively precious and used with caution as they require the most repairs and maintenance out of the common constructs. 
```


**Scratcher**
```
Nicknamed scratchers due to the sound of their small metal feet on the floors of Parvo, these rodent like constructs are small, usually foot sized constructs designed to mimic shuttle looms. They exist primarily to carry messages and are quite fast, with the ability to move through small gaps and zip along walls and gutters to get where they are going. 

They have decent intelligence, given that they need to navigate the complicated streets and deliver messages or small packages to specific individuals. They have eyes at both ends of their torpedo-like torsos, with six little legs that scurry along, creating their signature scratching noise as they move
```


**Howl**
```
Howls are the most intelligent constructs, and the most complicated and advanced of the common models. They are very similar to dogs (which are still common themselves in the city) with lithe muscular hound-like bodies and dangerous looking conical heads.

Many Guild enforcers utilize howls as guard dogs and enforcement tools, as they are especially suited to hunting down their targets in the narrow turns of the Bellows and the Stacks. As more complicated, and therefore more expensive designs, it's less common to see them in poorer neighbourhoods, although occasionally they can be seen used as work animals due to their surprising strength
