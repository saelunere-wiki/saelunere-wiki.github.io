Two kinds of rules live here.

**Table Rules** are the homebrew the players use at the table - read these to play.

**For the Archivist** are the rules the AI (the "archivist") follows when turning a
session recording into the notes and chapters on this site. They're kept in the
open so everyone can see how the archive is made: what counts as canon, how the
summaries and stories are written, and which writing style is being used.

> ⚠️ **These "For the Archivist" pages are experimental and AI-generated** -
> first drafts, not settled doctrine. **Suggestions are very welcome**, especially
> on writing style: which author to echo, whether each episode sticks to one
> character's point of view, past vs. present tense, first vs. third person, and so
> on. See **Style & Voice**. (These choices mostly affect the **Story** chapters;
> the **Summary** stays simple and factual.)
