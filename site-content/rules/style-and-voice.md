# Style & Voice

This page records **how the prose should read** — so everyone can see what style
the archive is in, and so it stays consistent across episodes.

> ⚠️ **Experimental and AI-generated.** Everything here is a first guess, not a
> rule set in stone. **Tell the GM or the archivist what you'd prefer** and it'll
> change. These choices shape the **Story** chapters; the **Summary** stays plain.

## Default voice

Clean, readable storybook prose: third person, past tense, scene-paced and
dialogue-driven. Clear over clever. This is the safe default and what the Story
chapters use unless a palette is chosen below.

**No em dashes.** Use a regular hyphen, a comma, or split into two sentences
instead. A house style preference, not up for debate per palette.

## Author palette (optional)

The prose can deliberately mimic a particular author's style — the previous
project experimented with this (a William Gibson–styled novel pass lives in the
old archive's `Gibson_experiment/` for reference). A palette changes *texture*
(sentence rhythm, vocabulary, imagery), never the **facts** — all of
[Canon & Discipline](#rules-canon-and-discipline) still applies.

If you adopt one, **record it here** so readers know what they're reading:

> **Current palette:** none — clean storybook prose (the default).

To change it, replace that line with, e.g. *"Current palette: hard-boiled noir,
short sentences, sensory grit,"* and write to that brief.

## Open style choices (suggestions welcome)

These are the dials we can set for the Story chapters. Nothing here is decided —
say the word and any of them changes:

- **Author palette** — echo a specific author's texture, or keep it neutral.
- **Point of view** — one fixed POV character per episode, or rotate between the
  party as scenes shift?
- **Person** — third person ("she crossed the Furlong") or first person ("I
  crossed the Furlong")?
- **Tense** — past tense ("the lock gave") or present tense ("the lock gives")?
- **Voice** — active by default ("Bri forced the hatch"); we generally avoid
  passive ("the hatch was forced") unless it suits a moment.
- **Register** — plain and clear, or lusher and more literary?

> **Current choices:** third person · past tense · active voice · neutral palette ·
> POV follows whoever is acting most in each scene. *(Defaults — change freely.)*

The **Summary** ignores all of this — it stays a simple, factual bullet list.

## Consistency

Whatever the palette, keep it the same from episode to episode. If the GM or
players want a different feel, change it here first, then write to the new brief —
don't drift mid-campaign without noting it.
