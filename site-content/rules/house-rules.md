# House Rules

## Skills

Two skills are split into specialisms. Whenever you have a chance to gain
proficiency in either, choose **which subset** you gain proficiency in.

### History

- **History (Ancient)** - the history of the world before [[The Theocide|theocide]],
  including its geography, society, and technology, and the study of things
  potentially "lost" following the fall.
- **History (Municipal)** - the history of [[Parvo]], focused on its
  transformation from an essence plant into the city it is today; includes the
  layout of the city, its districts, and its people.
- **History (Politics)** - up-to-date knowledge of who is who, which factions
  hold power where, and the connections, schemes, and areas of control within the
  city (see [[The City Council]]). *You may use Charisma instead of Intelligence
  for this skill if it fits the character.*

### Arcana

- **Arcana (Engineering)** - practical uses of magic, particularly essence
  machinery and magical technology, from everyday items to powerful artifacts.
  Since most "technology" has a magical element, this covers most machinery used
  in the city.
- **Arcana (Arcanography)** - magic as a science: understanding how magic and
  essence connect and work within the world. A lesser understanding of specific
  item usage, but a better understanding of why the systems function overall (see
  [[The College of Arcanographers]]).

## Spells

- **Detect Magic - Removed.** With magic far more prevalent and innately present
  in all beings, an *Arcana (Arcanography)* check functions in the same way as
  this spell and can be performed by anyone.

- **Revivify, Resurrection - Removed.** Death is permanent.

*(More changes and tweaks to certain spells may be coming.)*
