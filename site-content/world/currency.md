# Currency

- **1 Ducat** = 4 Marks
- **1 Mark** = 4 Pennies

## What things cost

An **essence core is worth 20 to 30 ducats**, sometimes more. It is the figure
everything else is measured against, and it is brutal set beside a wage: a
labourer earns about **1 ducat a shift**, so a single core is a month of a working
life, and ten of them in a box is more than a year's.

It is why stripping cores out of dead machinery is the whole economics of a
breakers' yard, and why [[The Corewright's Association]] licences anyone who
touches one.
