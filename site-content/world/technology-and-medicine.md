# Technology & Medicine

## Essence cores are ordinary
**Constructs and essence cores are everyday technology.** Nobody is surprised to see a core powering the most mundane thing imaginable - they are simply how the city works.

The economics matter: **buying a new essence core costs more than recycling an old one**, so **scrapping essence machines is commonplace**. Salvage, breaking, and recycling are ordinary trades, and a steady supply of dead machinery flows through the city to be picked apart.

## Medicine
Medicine is **quite advanced, because of magic**. An injury that would kill a person in a world without it is often survivable here, and the [[The Institute of Physikers]] are genuinely good at their work.

**Disease, however, is still a serious threat** - especially in bad environments, where damp, heat, and crowding do what they have always done. Good medicine does not compensate for a bad place to live.

## Prosthetics are limited
For all that, **prosthetics remain crude**. The technology to control essence-core machinery **through nerve endings does not exist**, so replacement limbs are simple, mechanical, and **unpowered**. Someone who loses an arm gets a functional tool, not a working hand.


## Essence firearms

Guild forces carry weapons that use an **essence core to drive a bolt** far harder
and faster than any bow or crossbow could. The **essence pistol** is the sidearm;
the **essence carbine** is the heavier shoulder weapon the
[[The Guild of Engineers|Guild of Engineers']] Guardsmen Brigade carries, and its
first shot can be overcharged before it needs to recharge. They are guild
equipment, and rare in anyone else's hands.

## Magic and who gets to use it
For the poorer parts of the city, the limits on magic are **material components and education** - not law, and not birth. Nothing stops a poor Parvosian from using magic. What stops them is that they cannot afford the materials and have no way to learn new spells. Building essence machinery is gated the same way: it takes knowledge *and* access to cores.

The aptitude itself is near-universal: **most people who try can manage basic cantrips.** Being entirely unable to work any magic at all is **rare**, and quietly humiliating in a city where magic is a trade like any other.

**Wild magic** - power that surges on its own, without being called - is a **rare occurrence, but not unheard of** in Parvo. Rare enough to cause trouble for whoever has it; common enough that the city has a word for it and is not astonished to meet one.

Magic in Parvo is therefore less a matter of talent than of means.

## What an essence weapon can and cannot do

Essence weapons are guild kit: a core drives a bolt far harder and faster than any
bow could, and every shot burns core, which is why they are expensive to own and
expensive to fire.

There are limits, and they are a matter of energy. To throw an essence blast **a
long distance and still land it accurately** takes enough power that it implies
**a great many essence cores and a great deal of money** - the difficulty is
keeping the projectile fast, steady and true over the distance.

It is not the sort of thing that gets built in the basement of an old house.
