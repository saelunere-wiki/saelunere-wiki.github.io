# Humans

Humans were the people of Saelunere before [[The Theocide]]. Since the death of
[[Aelia]] and the cry of the gods, no new humans have been born — every child
since has been either [[Aela]] or [[Darkin]].

Humans are therefore the older generation: those born before the corruption began
(the year 728 — see [[The Calendar & Seasons]]).
