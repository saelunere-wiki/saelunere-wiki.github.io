# Darkin

Darkin are one of the two races that emerged after the [[The Theocide|theocide]], appearing,
like the [[Aela]], seemingly at random. They also come in three forms, though
these are not seen to be tied to any particular source given the random nature of
their birth.

Some older folk distrust the Darkin and relate their more devilish features to
the evil aspects of [[Valar]]. Despite this, most simply see it as a quirk of
birth, much like the Aela.

Darkin typically exhibit three forms: some have a resistance to poison and
sickness, others greater control of and resistance to fire than normal, and
others still seem resistant to death itself. They bear a resemblance to "devils,"
with some growing horns, tails, tusks, and glowing eyes.
