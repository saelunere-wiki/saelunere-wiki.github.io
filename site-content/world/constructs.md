# Constructs

Constructs are commonplace around the city. Despite the availability of human
labour, constructs have greater strength, better mobility, and can be outfitted
for more specialized tasks. Most businesses and crews have one or two constructs
that they use, while the bigger outfits can operate and maintain hundreds at a
time.

Constructs are not sentient, and have a varying level of intelligence ranging
from something similar to livestock up to a well-trained workhorse or hound. In
most cases they have replaced beasts of burden and other animals that would take
up too much space or resources to keep in [[Parvo]].

They are run by Essence Cores, which are the remit of [[The Corewright's Association]].

## Common types

Although there are many variations and specializations, four types are seen in
day-to-day life.

### Bullyboy

Quadrupedal beasts of burden, most often likened to a horse or ox. They have
large bodies and four legs, with no head — instead, between the shoulders of their
front legs, they have a large circular disc that functions as a lamp and as all
the necessary sensory organs. They are the least intelligent constructs, usually
standing completely idle until given very simple commands, and are used for
haulage and as traction engines.

### Gladhand

Resembling something between spiders and crabs, roughly the size of a wash
basket, with ten legs arranged symmetrically around a circular torso and two
stalk-like antenna "eyes." With incredible dexterity, Gladhands are perfect for
climbing around buildings and navigating gantries and ceilings where humans
struggle. Their spindly limbs are fragile, making them relatively precious and
the most maintenance-hungry of the common constructs.

### Scratcher

Nicknamed for the sound of their small metal feet on Parvo's floors, these
rodent-like constructs are small — usually foot-sized — and exist primarily to
carry messages. They are quite fast, able to move through small gaps and zip along
walls and gutters, with decent intelligence to navigate the complicated streets.
They have eyes at both ends of their torpedo-like torsos and six little legs.

### Howl

The most intelligent and advanced of the common models, very similar to dogs
(which are still common in the city) with lithe, muscular hound-like bodies and
dangerous-looking conical heads. Many guild enforcers use Howls as guard dogs and
enforcement tools, especially suited to hunting targets through the narrow turns
of [[The Bellows]] and [[The Stacks]]. As expensive designs, they are less common
in poorer neighbourhoods.
