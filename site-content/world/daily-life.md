# Water & Hygiene

## Bathhouses
Not every home has running water, least of all in the poorer districts, [[The Stacks]]
above all. **Bathhouses fill the gap.** They are public places to wash, and much like
a tavern they are as much social and recreational as practical: somewhere to go and
somewhere to be seen, not only somewhere to get clean.

## Washing by magic
*Create Water* and *Prestidigitation* will both do the job, and neither is an exotic
spell. The limit is the usual one in Parvo, material components and education rather
than law or birth (see [[Technology & Medicine]]). For most people the bathhouse is
simpler.

## Drinking water is the scarcity
**Water for washing is not hard to come by. Water safe to drink is.** Filling a tub,
doing the laundry, sluicing a floor: none of that is a struggle, and nobody thinks
twice about the quality of what they use for it. What [[The Water Company]] sells,
and what a poor household boils waste water to substitute for, is water you can drink.
