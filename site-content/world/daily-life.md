# Water & Hygiene

Not every home has running water, especially in poorer districts. **Bathhouses**
fill that gap — public places to wash up that double as a social and
recreational spot, much like a tavern.

Wizards and clerics with access to *Create Water* or *Prestidigitation* can
also conjure or clean themselves up magically, though that's the exception
rather than the norm for most people.

**Drinking water is the real scarcity, not water in general.** Getting water
for washing, laundry, and similar chores is easy enough — you just wouldn't
want to drink it untreated.
