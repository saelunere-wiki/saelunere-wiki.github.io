# Education

Schooling in Parvo is short, early, and overwhelmingly practical. Almost everyone's real education happens on the job.

## The working classes - apprenticeship
Through the primary and secondary years (**to about age twelve**), a working-class child trains in the family's trade or a local business - the forge, the crew, the shop, the pipes. There is little formal classroom time, and **the quality varies enormously** depending on the job and the household. After twelve, it is work.

## The middle classes and above - tutoring, then shadowing
Children of merchants, guild management, and the great houses get **tutored classes until about ten to twelve**. From twelve onward it is still very hands-on: **shadowing people in their roles**, learning the business on site, walked through the trade by those who already hold it.

## The College of Arcanographers
The best education in the city - at least for anything magical-leaning - and the hardest to reach. **Slots are limited and there are fees**, so places go to wealthier families, with the **occasional rare scholarship** for an exceptional student of lesser means. Children of College faculty also get a **discounted place** - one of the few routes in that is neither wealth nor a scholarship.


## The Divine Calling
Those who join [[The Divine Calling]] enter a **monastic religious education, usually between eight and ten years old**, and are expected to **serve for life**. Because the order's focus is medicine, that schooling is arguably **better than anything the middle classes and below can otherwise get**.
