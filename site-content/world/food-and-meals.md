# Food & Meals

Space, not soil, is the constraint: Parvo has to feed itself inside a walled city.

## What's on the table
- **Pork and chicken**, with **eggs**, are the common protein - all easy to keep in small spaces.
- **Vegetables and beans** are the staples, rather than broad-acre crops like wheat or corn, which need land the city simply doesn't have.
- **Most houses keep a vegetable plot** of some kind - a balcony bed up in [[The Stacks]], a yard row in [[The Core|the Core]].

## Food by magic
Magic can simply make food - **Create Food and Water**, **Goodberry**, and for grand occasions **Heroes' Feast** - and it can produce very nearly anything. But it is no everyday solution: few people know the spells, and those who do must spend the slots and components to cast them. Conjured meals are therefore a thing of **special occasions**, celebrations, and emergencies - not the daily table.
