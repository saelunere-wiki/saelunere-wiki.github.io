# Geography & Cosmology

Saelunere literally is an orb floating in a space-like void. The firmament is a
solid spherical barrier of force that keeps the water, landmass, and atmosphere
of the world within it. This is understood to be fact by almost everyone, and so
while in the past an explorer or two has set out into the surrounding sea to
reach the firmament, most readily understand this is simply reality and that
there is nothing to be gained by attempting to explore further.

There is no sun or moon in the sky. Light simply grows over the course of the day
and diminishes again, and night is always very dark, with no moon or other light
source. Stars are visible in the sky far off in the void, though nothing is known
of their purpose or origin.

For how time is reckoned in this world, see [[The Calendar & Seasons]].
