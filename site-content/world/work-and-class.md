# Work & Class

Parvo sorts roughly into the labouring classes, a merchant-and-management middle, and the guild elite above them.

## The working classes
The bulk of the city. Common trades are the **maintenance of machines**, small-plot **farming**, **manufacturing** and **crafting**, **hauling and transport**, and **hospitality** - taverns, entertainment, and the like.

Above all: **a very large share of the entire workforce does one thing - maintaining the old essence systems that keep the city running.** Parvo is a machine that has to be kept alive. Its pipes, engines, and cores demand constant attention, and most working hands in the city are somewhere in that effort. It is the world [[Theodore Blackwood]] rose out of, starting as a pipe-maintenance worker in [[The Bellows]].

## The middle classes
Chiefly **merchants and traders**. Because resources are so scarce, whoever moves and allots them holds real power - merchants here are far more influential than their equivalents would be in a richer world. Alongside them sits the guild **"management" tier**: the supervisors, clerks, and officers who run guild business day to day.

## Above
Guild leadership and the old propertied houses, all out in [[The Outlines|the Outlines]]: the guild leaders live atop their guilds' [[The Spires|Spires]], and the other great houses in mansions alongside them.
