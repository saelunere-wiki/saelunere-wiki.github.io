# The Faith of the Four Kin

*The origins of Saelunere, as told by the Faith of the Four Kin.*

In the beginning there was a black orb, floating in an endless white void. Four
siblings found the orb.

**Aelia** pierced the orb with a spear of pure light, golden in radiance, and the
hole she made flooded the orb with a beautiful dawn. But the light shone on
nothingness, and had no purpose.

So **Erethan** fired his bow into the orb, the shaft a curling vine, the arrow tip
a blossoming bud, and as the arrow struck the ground inside, great fonts of life
sprung forth across the surface. But the life was parched and dry, and could not
flourish.

So **Salador** struck the orb with a hammer of pure water, each impact sending
crashing waves forth, bringing water to nourish the life within. But the waves
froze to ice, the rays of light not enough to fight the crippling cold of the
void.

So **Yuna** slashed a sword of flame through the orb, and many fires burst into
existence where her blade touched. The warmth of the fires thawed the ice and
brought balance, allowing cycles to begin.

The siblings were happy with their work and entered the orb, where they created
the creatures of the world and guided them to flourish.

But **Valar**, their cousin, had grown jealous of their creation. He stole his way
across the void and placed his hand over the hole that Aelia had pierced,
creating night, darkness, and poisoning the minds within with evil.

The four siblings, now indebted to the world they had created, left to challenge
Valar, fighting back against his influence as best they can. Aelia fights daily
to bring the light of day, Erethan creates life to outpace the endless march of
death, Salador pushes back the tides that he once created, and Yuna warms the ice
cold frost to bring the harvest seasons.

## The Four Kin

- **Aelia** - the warrior of light; the mother goddess. Slain in [[The Theocide]].
- **Erethan** - bringer of life, set against death.
- **Salador** - master of water and the tides.
- **Yuna** - bearer of flame, warmth, and the harvest.
- **Valar** - the jealous cousin; origin of night, darkness, and evil.

The four seasons are named for the Kin - see [[The Calendar & Seasons]].
