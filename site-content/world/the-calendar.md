# The Calendar & Seasons

- **Current year:** 756
- **The corruption began:** 728 (after the death of [[Aelia]] — see [[The Theocide]])
- **Year length:** 400 days
- **Each season:** 100 days

## Seasons

| Season | Time of year | Named for |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| Aela   | Spring       | The mother goddess [[Aelia]] |
| Ember  | Summer       | The fires of [[Yuna]] |
| Terr   | Autumn       | [[Erethan]]'s earth |
| Tide   | Winter       | The storms of [[Salador]] |

## Dates

Dates are written as **[Season] [Day]** — for example, *Aela 22* is the 22nd day
of Aela.

## Festival Days (Day 00)

Each season opens with a festival on its zeroth day:

| Festival | Day | Theme |
|----------|-----|-------|
| Everbring  | Aela 00  | Growth and Life |
| Highbreath | Ember 00 | Joy and Freedom |
| Harvestfest| Terr 00  | Plenty and Abundance |
| Frostmorn  | Tide 00  | Remembrance |
