# Language & Signs

**Parvosi** is the language of the city - the common tongue. No other language is spoken in [[Parvo]]; there are no foreign tongues to learn and no dialects worth the name.

What Parvo has instead are **trade codes**: ways of communicating where speech will not carry. They are occupational rather than secret, and anyone who works the relevant trade picks them up as a matter of course.

## Technic

An **engineering notation** used to blueprint and label essence machinery -
technical notes on specific objects, items and machines, such as serial numbers.
It is the working script of [[The Guild of Engineers]] and of anyone who builds,
maintains or breaks essence machines. Many machine workers can recognise it;
reading and writing it properly is a trained skill. It is written, not spoken.

## Noma

Someone who cannot use magic at all, from "no magic". Most people who try can manage a
cantrip or two, whether they learn it the wizard's way or it is born in them, so a noma
is uncommon rather than unheard of. There is a mild stigma to it - a child in the magic
class who cannot do the magic gets teased - but it amounts to little later, beyond being
shut out of the trades that need spellcraft. [[Felix Klaudius]] was born one, and
[[Annie Buckman]] is one.
