# The Brassline

A small haulage and courier outfit working out of a depot at the bottom of
[[The Stacks]]. Like every courier crew in Parvo, they move goods **on foot**.

It is run by [[Mira]], who founded it and no longer goes out on deliveries
herself, keeping an office above the depot floor instead.

## How it works

The business is built on **set routes** - standing arrangements to collect here
and deliver there on a regular cycle. A courier will run the same route on the
same day of the week for as long as they hold it. [[Lark]] ran one every Aelsdae,
from one side of the city to the other.

Couriers can sleep at the depot; there are bunks for those who need them.

## The pin

Brassline couriers carry **a pin naming the outfit**, worn to identify who they
work for. Handing it back is how you leave.

Lark handed hers to Mira two days after [[Theodore Blackwood]] was killed.
