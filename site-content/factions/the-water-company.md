# The Water Company

Undoubtedly one of the most important guilds in the city, the Water Company
strives to remain neutral amid the factionalism of [[The City Council]], in order
to ensure that the population and industry which need a steady supply can receive
it. Nonetheless, it is a thankless job, given the scarcity of large volumes of
clean water in the current age.

- **Director:** [[Samuel Greaves]]
