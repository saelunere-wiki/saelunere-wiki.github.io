# The Artificer's Union

The Artificer's Union represents the many businesses and tradespeople who create,
deal in, or repair essence objects. Its spokesperson changes often, as different
companies grow and wane in power. It holds a seat on [[The City Council]].

Currently, two companies have formed an uneasy coalition to represent the
artificers of the city:

- **Torman & Sons** — [[Harriford Torman]]
- **The Longfellow Company** — [[Julie Longfellow]]
