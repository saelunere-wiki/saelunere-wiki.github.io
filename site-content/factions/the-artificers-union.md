# The Artificer's Union

The Artificer's Union represents the many businesses and tradespeople who create,
deal in, or repair essence objects. Its spokesperson changes often, as different
companies grow and wane in power. It holds a seat on [[The City Council]].

Currently, two companies have formed an uneasy coalition to represent the
artificers of the city:

- **Torman & Sons** - [[Harriford Torman]]
- **The Longfellow Company** - [[Julie Longfellow]]

## The Spire

The Artificers' Spire looks less like a building than a machine stood on end. A central shaft runs the height of it, and everything else has been added onto that shaft over the years: rooms cobbled on where rooms were wanted, pipework threaded across the outside, grills, gantries and stair runs bolted wherever access was needed. **Steam vents** let go along its whole length at intervals, and smoke and steam roll off it constantly, so the tower is half-obscured by its own weather.

Nothing about it is finished, each time the chair changes hands the new holder changes the rules, and what the parts of the tower are used for changes with them - wings repurposed, floors given over to a different trade, additions bolted on and additions abandoned where they stand. It works loudly, all day, and never quite settles into a final shape: a **vertical factory** in constant motion, and a record of every coalition the Union has ever had.

## Formation

Unlike the guilds that carried over from before [[The Theocide]], the Union was built by newcomers. It gathered out of the artificers and traders who arrived among the refugees and found their goods and services in high demand in a city that had lost so much. They grouped together to **bargain collectively** and to help one another get fledgling businesses onto their feet.

That beginning still shapes it. The Union has always been an alliance of separate businesses rather than one body with one voice, which is why the seat it holds passes between whichever companies are strongest at the time.
