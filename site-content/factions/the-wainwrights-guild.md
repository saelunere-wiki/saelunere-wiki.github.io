# The Wainwright's Guild

An up-and-coming faction of [[The Porter's Guild]], the Wainwrights deal with
wheels and carts. Having recently overseen the implementation of the Circular —
the tram that rings [[The Furlong]] — they now hold a decent amount of control and
oversight over how people and goods traverse [[Parvo]].

- **Leader:** [[Orson Knight]]

## The Spire

The Wainwrights' Spire is a shorter tower than many of the more prominent guilds, but set into the top is an enormous **ornate wheel**, and the wheel turns: it hoists a large, equally ornate elevator up the outside of the tower in full view of the street. Nothing about the arrangement is necessary, it serves as a demonstration, mounted where the whole district can watch it.

The rest of the tower is decorated to the same pitch. Wheels and circles are everywhere - in the stonework, the railings, the window heads, the doors - the motif repeated at every scale. Most Parvosians think it gaudy and overdone.

## Formation

Formed out of the haulers and cartwrights who arrived with the refugee caravans - and who discovered almost immediately that a trade built for the open road was worth far less inside a city this compact. They have been one of the smallest guilds by membership ever since.

That has made them restless rather than resigned. The Wainwrights have always hunted for new services to offer, looking to make their membership matter to the wider city in ways that carts alone never would. [[The Circular|The Circular]] is the most successful of those attempts by a distance.

## Wheels and feet

Goods that once crossed the city on a porter's back increasingly go by wagon and cart instead - more per trip, fewer hands, cheaper. Anyone who watches the Furlong can see it happening.
