# The City Council

Issues, laws, and policy in [[Parvo]] are decided by the leaders of its most
powerful guilds, known as the City Council. Officially there are seven
representatives from the major guilds, though meetings are often attended by
minor guild leaders and other representatives when expertise or informed opinion
is required.

Parvosians pride themselves on a meritocracy — the idea that those most suited to
leading the city rise through the guilds to earn powerful voices on the council.
In theory, when an issue pertains to the expertise of a particular guild, their
arguments should carry greater weight and the debate should usually go in their
favour.

## The seven major guilds

- [[The Guild of Engineers]]
- [[The Institute of Physikers]]
- [[The Artificer's Union]]
- [[The College of Arcanographers]]
- [[The Craftwork Consortium]]
- [[The Porter's Guild]]
- [[The Water Company]]

Not everyone is content with rule by the largest guilds — [[Theodore Blackwood]]
campaigns to replace the council with a truly democratic, elected government.
