Power in [[Parvo]] is held by its **guilds**. The seven greatest send their
leaders to [[The City Council]], which sets the city's laws and policy - a proud
meritocracy where, in theory, the guild with the most relevant expertise carries
the debate.

Beneath the seven majors sit smaller sub-guilds and specialist offshoots, and
beyond the guild system entirely are other powers - like the gangs that rule the
tunnels of [[The Bellows]].
