# The Craftwork Consortium

Despite being an essential part of city life, the various industries, businesses,
and workshops that make up the Consortium are often looked down on for the
"mundane" nature of their craft. Nonetheless, each industry within tends to hold a
lucrative niche, employ many people, and earn good income - giving the Consortium
decent sway in city politics and a seat on [[The City Council]].

- **Toolmaker's Guild** - [[Tora Hargrave]]
- **The Order of Smiths** - [[Maximilian Dulwich]]

## The Spire

The Consortium's Spire is the plainest of them: square corners, flat faces, and large windows in even rows from the ground to the top. There is almost no ornament on the structure itself. It was built to be useful and to be filled.

Each floor belongs to a different craft, which uses it as a headquarters and offices rather than a workshop - the actual making happens down in [[The Core|the Core]], among the yards and forges. The trades dress their own windows, though, and that is where the building gives itself away: window boxes, hangings and glazing differ floor by floor, and anyone with an eye for it can stand in the street and read off which craft sits where.

Toward the top the tower steps inward to a point, and those upper steps - eight floors of them at least - are given over to residences for prominent members of the trades below.

## Formation

Came together much as [[The Artificer's Union]] did - refugee craftspeople banding into a body that could speak for them all - but with a difference that mattered: they folded in a good part of Parvo's **existing** workforce, the people already running the forges and the mundane craft workshops. Premises, tools and custom were there from the start, so the Consortium established itself early and firmly, above all in [[The Core]], where its yards and workshops still stand.
