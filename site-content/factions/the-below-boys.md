# The Below Boys

A gang of upstarts who have started charging tolls to those travelling through
the underground spaces of [[The Bellows]].

## The rumour

In the days after [[Theodore Blackwood]] was shot, a story went round the city
that he had **criminal connections**, that a gang had been involved, and that the
Below Boys were behind it. It reached the forges and the Stacks within two days,
and was repeated to [[Calder Flynn]] by his own parents.

There is nothing behind it. No one repeating it has produced a name or a reason,
and those who knew Theo dismiss it outright.
