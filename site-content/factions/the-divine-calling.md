# The Divine Calling

The divine arm of [[The Institute of Physikers]]. As devout kinsworn, its members
focus more heavily on understanding the gods, as well as espousing peace,
tranquility, and reflection in the name of [[The Faith of the Four Kin|the Four Kin]].

- **Father:** [[Father Manston Ulysses]]

## The Spire

The Calling's Spire is a smaller building than most, and it is not one tower but **four** - four thin spires rising together, linked at intervals by suspended bridges that cross between them at different heights. From the ground the effect is of a single structure opened up: in places you can see straight through it.

The style is old and devotional, closer to a cathedral than a guild house. Tall narrow **lancet windows** set with stone **tracery**; **quatrefoils** - four-lobed openings - worked through the stonework and the bridge rails; **crockets** running like curled leaves up every edge; carved **grotesques** at the gutter lines throwing the rain clear of the walls.

Each of the four spires tapers to a sharp **finial**, and each stands exactly the height of the other three. Not one of them rises above its neighbours.

## Formation

Like the Institute it joined, the Calling was already there. It came out of an existing organised branch of kinsworn who used divine magic charitably to heal, and [[The Theocide]] changed the scale of that work without changing its nature - charity and healing, as before.

It does not use its position to press religion on the city. Anyone who wishes to join its ranks and swear to uphold its principles is accepted, and it leaves the matter there.
