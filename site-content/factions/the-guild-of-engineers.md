# The Guild of Engineers

The Guild of Engineers is one of the biggest in [[Parvo]], founded originally from
the engineering and maintenance department of the Parvo essence plant. They
encompass everything related to essence machinery in the city — from maintenance
and renewal to innovation — and hold a seat on [[The City Council]].

- **Head Engineer:** [[Dr. Mannsfield T. Tammerton]]
- **Sub-faction:** [[The Corewright's Association]]
- **Notable member:** [[Theodore Blackwood]], Chief Essence Transfer Supervisor
