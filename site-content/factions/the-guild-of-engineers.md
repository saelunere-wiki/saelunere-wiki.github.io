# The Guild of Engineers

The Guild of Engineers is one of the biggest in [[Parvo]], and the same guild that
built and ran the original essence plant before [[The Theocide]] - it has carried
the name unchanged. They encompass everything related to essence machinery in the
city - from maintenance and renewal to innovation - and hold a seat on
[[The City Council]].

- **Head Engineer:** [[Dr. Mannsfield T. Tammerton]]
- **Sub-faction:** [[The Corewright's Association]]
- **Notable member:** [[Theodore Blackwood]], Chief Essence Transfer Supervisor

## Formation

The Engineers did not form after [[The Theocide]] so much as carry on through it. The Guild of Engineers already existed, under that name, running the essence plant and the works that heated the highland settlements around it - not a body of maintenance hands but the engineers who had designed, built and extended the whole apparatus. Maintenance was simply the largest part of the job.

When the rain turned and the refugees came, those were the same people who sealed the plant and kept the machinery alive. Nobody else understood how the place actually worked. That knowledge made them indispensable from the first day, and they have never since had to argue the point - it is the plain fact of the arrangement, and it is what cemented them as a vital part of Parvo's infrastructure.

## The Spire

The Engineers' [[The Spires|Spire]] is the **tallest building in Parvo**. It clears the next tallest by a few feet - not enough to look like boasting and too little to be an accident. It climbs in **stepped tiers**, each set back from the one below, tapering as it rises so that from the street it reads as one unbroken vertical line.

Pale stone and dark ironwork interweave all the way up: deep ribs and flutes run the full height between thick corner piers. The ornament is practical rather than heraldic, flutes of dim light highlight the stonework once night falls with a gentle glow. Decorative, and not for a moment gaudy: every panel is deliberate.

Despite this, it is clearly a **working building**. Vent stacks and service galleries are left plainly visible where another guild might have buried them - the whole tower carries the low constant sound of machinery through its stone. Imposing and grandiose, yet retaining the function of being the hub of engineering in the city.

The topmost level is an **open-air plaza**, flagged and balustraded, open to the sky at the highest point in the city - and **shielded by magic**. Protected from wind and weather, the rain stops at a line you cannot see, and Parvo lies underneath from the curtain wall to the chimney. It is the guild's one extravagant gesture, and nobody who has been up there forgets it. The Head Engineer's quarters occupy the levels beneath it.
