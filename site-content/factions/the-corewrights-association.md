# The Corewright's Association

A sub-department of [[The Guild of Engineers]], the Corewright's Association deals
with the creation and maintenance of Essence Cores — the devices that power and
run [[Constructs]]. It is a powerful faction, given the essential nature of
constructs in the city and the more "mysterious" quality of their functionality.

- **Corewright:** [[Kerwin Aberford]]

## The Spire

The Corewrights' Spire is squat and broad, made of dark stone - wide at the base. It sits shorter than many other spires and solid on its ground, and its walls are thick enough that the windows feel like slots cut into them.

There is little ornament on it. What decoration it has is cut shallow into the stone and easy to miss by day, when the tower looks plain to the point of grim.

At night it is a different building entirely. **Essence-powered flutes** run its face, and their dim glow picks the stonework out of the dark in a way the daylight never manages - and behind every slot window the labs and machinariums are still working. They do not stop. The tower burns quietly through the small hours, lit from within and traced from without.

At the top the mass draws suddenly in and tapers to a single thin spire, barbed the whole length with gothic decoration. The Association's head family live in the quarters within the tapered point.

## Formation

Constructs were at work in Parvo long before [[The Theocide]], and with them a body of employed corewrights. Afterwards they organised into a guild of their own - but **under** the Engineers rather than beside them. The two trades are so entwined that setting up a rival guild made sense to nobody; they chose instead to stay allied, sharing knowledge and expertise on anything touching essence machinery. That bargain is the one they still keep.

Any business that works commercially with cores or constructs - a repair shop, a breaker's yard, a haulage outfit running its own machines - does so **under a Corewright licence**, renewed for a fee. (On dues and enforcement generally, see [[Law & Crime]].)
