# The Institute of Physikers

The Institute is the source of training and research in all things medical.
Physikers serve an important and respected role as doctors providing care to the
sick and injured, and are at the forefront of research into the effects of
magical corruption. The guild holds a seat on [[The City Council]].

- **Surgeon General:** [[Dr. Maria Blackwell]]
- **Sub-faction:** [[The Divine Calling]]

## The Spire

The Institute's tower is built in the same old devotional style as much of Parvo's older stonework, but kept scrubbed and in good repair - the carving is sharp-edged rather than weathered, and nothing on it is allowed to crumble.

It widens as it rises. Overhanging balconies and box windows are **corbelled** out from the upper floors to win back floor space, so wings and wards sit above rooms narrower than themselves, and the tower spreads toward the top - reminiscent of a tree.

The top is flat, and it is a **garden**: rows of herbs and medicinal crops grown by the Institute for its own use. A tall wall runs the whole rim of it, blank and monastic and higher than a person, so that everyone up there - gardeners and walking patients alike - sees only the garden and the sky. The city is deliberately not part of the view.

## Formation

A necessity, and one of the first bodies established after [[The Theocide]]. Medical professionals and carers of every walk came together simply to deal with the fallout, and what began as an emergency response was formalised early into an institution for training and building the city's medical expertise. It has existed without a break ever since.
