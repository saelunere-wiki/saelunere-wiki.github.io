# The Porter's Guild

The Porter's Guild represents traders and couriers of all types within [[Parvo]],
especially when it comes to hauling goods on foot — still one of the most
efficient ways to move cargo through the dense city. The other guilds strive to
stay on the Porter's good side, as that can mean quicker routes and shorter
queues. The guild holds a seat on [[The City Council]].

- **Headman:** [[Sasha Langford]]
- **Sub-faction:** [[The Wainwright's Guild]]
