# The Abandoned Warehouse

The place where [[Felix Klaudius|Felix]] was held for three years, in
[[The Core|the Core]] on the edge of [[The Furlong]], tied to no guild that he knows
of. He never learned who held him: he heard a woman's voice, mostly, and once a man's.

He got out about a year ago with [[Theodore Blackwood]]'s help, killing a creature of
flesh and machine on the way and running from a second. When he and Theo went back a
few weeks later, or sent someone, it had been cleared out, and it has stood abandoned
since.
