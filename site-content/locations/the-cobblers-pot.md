# The Cobbler's Pot

A busy, working-class tavern in [[The Stacks]], not far from [[Salador's Slip]], in a
building that was once a cobbler's shop. The old trade was never taken down: many of
the tools and the shoe horns are still on the walls. People come and go all day,
enough of them that a group at a corner table draws no looks, and it is not the kind
of house that pays much for a musician. A beer is a penny.

## Episode 7

The five met here in the afternoon of Tide 05, after the fight at
[[Claringbold's Yard]], and talked over what to do next.
