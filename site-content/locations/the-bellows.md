# The Bellows

The lowest level of [[Parvo]], below ground level, is a warren of pipework, steam
vents, and essence engines, originally designed to give the industrial areas
topside easy access to the city's facilities.

Since space is now at a premium, many nooks, crannies, and tunnels have become
residential areas. The difficult navigation and maze-like layout naturally favour
criminals and those who would rather not be found. Even so, many engineers and
maintenance workers live in the Bellows simply to be close to their work.

More recently, a gang of upstarts calling themselves [[The Below Boys]] have
started charging tolls to those travelling through the underground spaces.
