# The Firebox

A boxing gym in [[The Stacks]]. [[Hollis]] gave it as his address in the employee
records at [[Claringbold's Yard]]; whether he lives there is not known.

## Episode 8

[[Alfie]] told Billiam that Hollis is keeping his share of the cores from Shrapnel's
safe here, and is looking for a buyer. The party has still not been.
