# The Core

The ring of districts between [[The Stacks]] and [[The Furlong]] — the industrial
heart of [[Parvo]] and, for most people, the ordinary middle of the city.

It is neither poor nor grand: workshops, yards, warehouses and government
buildings sit alongside decent housing, and a great deal of the city's actual
work happens here. Air and light are better than in the Stacks and worse than in
[[The Outlines]].

Where the Stacks are built upward for want of room, the Core simply spreads.
