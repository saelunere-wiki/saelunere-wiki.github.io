# The Circular

The essence-powered tram that runs the length of [[The Furlong]], carried on poles above the road in a constant one-way loop of carriages. It moves both passengers and cargo over the congestion below, and is overseen by [[The Wainwright's Guild]], who built it. Carriages do not properly stop so much as slow at boarding points, and riding it is an ordinary part of crossing the city - for those who can pay the fare.
