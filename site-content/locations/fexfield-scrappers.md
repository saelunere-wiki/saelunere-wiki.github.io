# Fexfield Scrappers

A scrapyard in [[The Core|the Core]], and one of the two names still legible in
[[Theodore Blackwood]]'s ledger.

[[Billiam Buckman|Billiam]] knows it as one of [[Claringbold's Yard]]'s
competitors, and it sits mid-Core, towards the Corewrights and the Guild of
Engineers: **a more professional outfit, with twenty or thirty staff**, running
an industrial scrapping operation rather than the scavenging and salvage
Claringbold's does.

In the ledger its line carries a couple of serial numbers and **x50**.

## The yard

Not a yard so much as a **big brick warehouse**, two or three storeys, with a yard
out back. Customers use the **front door**, under a sign reading *Fexfield
Scrappers*; the workers come in through the yard. Inside is one big open floor,
everything racked out and organised, machines being scrapped down and plenty of
people at work - nothing like the piles at Claringbold's. A [[The man in the apron|big man in an apron]]
works the floor by the front door. Upstairs is more workshop
than office.

It is a family business: [[Lucile Fexfield]] runs it with her brother, and their
father ran it before them.

## Episode 5

Billiam and Felix came in by the front door looking for work. Once Lucile knew they
were Shrapnel's, she closed the door and told them what she knew: Shrapnel's
contracts off the books, the Porter's Guild as buyers, and the two agents who had
called that morning and been sent away. She **hired Felix** to go through her books.
