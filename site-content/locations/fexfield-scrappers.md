# Fexfield Scrappers

A scrapyard in [[The Core|the Core]], and one of the two names still legible in
[[Theodore Blackwood]]'s ledger.

[[Billiam Buckman|Billiam]] knows it as one of [[Claringbold's Yard]]'s
competitors: **a more professional outfit, with twenty or thirty staff**, running
an industrial scrapping operation rather than the scavenging and salvage
Claringbold's does.

In the ledger its line carries a couple of serial numbers and **x50**.
