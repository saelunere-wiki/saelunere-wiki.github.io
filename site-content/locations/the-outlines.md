# The Outlines

Everything between [[The Furlong]] and the outer curtain wall — the outermost and
most desirable ring of [[Parvo]], where the air is clearest and furthest from the
chimney's runoff.

The guild [[The Spires]] stand here, along with the mansions of the propertied
houses. It is quieter and greener than anywhere further in, and the distance from
the centre is the point: wealth in Parvo is measured in how far you live from the
smoke.
