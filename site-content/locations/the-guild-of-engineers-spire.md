# The Guild of Engineers' Spire

One of [[The Spires]] near Parvo's outer wall, housing the offices of [[The Guild of Engineers]], and the tallest building in the city. From here, [[Theodore Blackwood]] runs his political campaign for democracy. *(The building itself - its architecture and its shielded rooftop plaza - is described on [[The Guild of Engineers]]; kept in one place so the two cannot drift.)*
