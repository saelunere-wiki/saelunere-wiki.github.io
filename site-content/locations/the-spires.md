# The Spires

The tall, thin towers standing in [[The Outlines]] near [[Parvo]]'s outer wall,
where the air is as clear and cool as it can be while still protected by the city
walls.

**A spire is a guild's headquarters** - broadly one per major guild - and each is
at once a place of business and a residence. Guild offices fill the working
floors, connected by complicated mechanical or essence-powered elevators, while
the **guild leader and their family live in the quarters at the top**.

Because ground-level space is at such a premium, most spires are very thin -
taking the footprint of a large family house at most - using masterful building
techniques to reach seemingly impossible heights. They are architecturally
beautiful, themed to the character of the guild that raised them and designed as
a statement to its peers, some featuring highly decorative facades and bright
paintwork.

The rest of the city's rich and powerful - the propertied houses who do not
*lead* a guild - do not build spires. They live in **mansions** out in
[[The Outlines]] alongside them.

The offices of [[The Guild of Engineers]] occupy one such spire - where
[[Theodore Blackwood]] keeps his political campaign.
