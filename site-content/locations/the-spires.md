# The Spires

Dotted around the outside of [[Parvo]], as close to the edge as can be managed,
the rich and powerful build tall towers. Here the air is as clear and cool as it
can be while still protected by the city walls. These spires act not only as
opulent residences but as fully fledged estates and places of business for the
guild leaders, merchant classes, and political powers of Parvo.

A spire is usually laid out with residences for the family at the top, then guest
rooms, living quarters, and amenities, with servants' quarters, infrastructure,
and business offices lower still. Many use complicated mechanical or
essence-powered elevators to ensure ease of access across the many floors.

Because ground-level space is at such a premium, most spires are very thin —
taking the footprint of a large family house at most — using masterful building
techniques to reach seemingly impossible heights. They are architecturally
beautiful, themed to the liking of their owners and designed as a statement to
their peers, some featuring highly decorative facades and bright paintwork.

The offices of [[The Guild of Engineers]] occupy one such spire — where
[[Theodore Blackwood]] keeps his political campaign.
