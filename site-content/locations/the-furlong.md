# The Furlong

The Furlong is a ring road that travels around the outside of the chimney stack
and its associated plant buildings - the main route through [[Parvo]], where most
traffic is forced to pass to reach the inner or outer areas of the city's
circular shape.

Government buildings, city infrastructure, and large warehouses tend to lie
directly roadside, where they need constant access or process the greatest volume
of traffic. Many pedestrian bridges and tunnels cross over and under the Furlong,
so for many residents and businesses it is quicker to have porters or constructs
haul goods on foot than to join the thoroughfare.

Many joke that the Furlong is technically an infinite traffic jam - two lines of
slow-moving carts and trucks connected end to end, forever circling the city.
Only in the very early hours does the volume die down enough to leave visible
gaps.

## The Circular

Around the circumference of the Furlong, built on poles above it, runs the
**Circular** - an essence-powered tram originally designed for coal carts, since
modified to carry passengers as well as cargo to avoid the traffic below. It is a
ring of carriages in constant motion, requiring people to hop on and off, which
makes it unsuitable for large, bulky, or delicate cargo. Its recent
implementation is overseen by [[The Wainwright's Guild]].
